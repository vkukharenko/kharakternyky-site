Nayrin

The Road Home

About the book

Nayrin was seventeen when a nuclear catastrophe destroyed his familiar world and separated him from his younger brother, Zairin. Stranded in an unfamiliar world, he must learn to survive among people whose lives resemble a distant past. His journey brings new friends, dangerous pursuers and abilities he never knew he possessed. But his purpose never changes: to find his brother.
