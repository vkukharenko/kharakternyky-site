MILITARY COMMAND · ASSAULT ON THE SETTLEMENTS

Lord Alister

A commander of armoured groups leading the assault on defensive perimeters and settlements.

PROFILE

Commander of armoured groups

Lord Alister is one of the commanders serving under the Supreme. He directs armoured units during the assault on settlements and coordinates operations with Lord Dark. His decisions respond to changing conditions on the battlefield.

Title: Lord

Role: Military commander

Primary command: Armoured groups and ground assault

Coordinates with: The Supreme and Lord Dark

DAY 17 · BREAKING THE PERIMETER

Striking the defences

After the defensive systems have been weakened, the Supreme orders Alister and Lord Dark to commit their armoured units and attack from different directions. Alister takes responsibility for the southern and eastern sectors. He relies on previously identified landing points and routes rather than concentrating the assault into a single massed strike.

DAY 18 · THE ARMOURED WITHDRAWAL

Decisions under pressure

When the rear armoured groups suffer losses, vehicles are disabled and communications with units become unreliable, Alister changes the mission of the forward forces. He orders them to link up with units already fighting in the settlements, stop advancing independently and move only with support from neighbouring groups. At the same time, he organises collection points, separates serviceable vehicles from damaged equipment and works to restore communications.
