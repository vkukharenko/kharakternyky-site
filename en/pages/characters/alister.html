CANONICAL APPEARANCE

LORD ALISTER

One of the Council's lords

Alister belongs to the circle of lords summoned by the Supreme to discuss the appearance of Zero-worlders near their domains. He is among those who question whether immediate intervention in the Dark One's war with the Zero-worlders is necessary.

Status: Council lord.

Position: Cautious about intervening in the war again.

Connection to events: Takes part in the council meeting after Dmytro, Simon and their group infiltrate the Dark One's domain.

COUNCIL OF LORDS

Before a new war

During the meeting, the Supreme reminds the lords of the old war with the Zero-worlders, their losses and the reasons the current council exists. After viewing live recordings from the Dark One's domain, the lords agree to a joint operation.
