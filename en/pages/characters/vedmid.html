PROFILE

An infantryman with frontline experience

Vedmid (literally “Bear”) is a Ukrainian infantryman who has fought since the beginning of the war. In the frontline episode, he operates alongside Phoenix and other soldiers during a dangerous mission into the grey zone.

CHARACTER AND CONDUCT

Reserved, decisive and reliable

The supplied visual dossier describes Vedmid as reserved, decisive, reliable and straightforward, with little tolerance for chaos. In the manuscript episode, he is shown as an experienced infantryman who responds quickly to changing battlefield conditions and follows orders.

During the mission into the grey zone, Vedmid meets the reinforcements, prepares his equipment and moves around the position on Phoenix's order. After Ihor is killed, he freezes for a brief moment in shock, then continues with the group.

ROLE IN THE STORY

A frontline mission with Phoenix

Vedmid takes part in an operation near abandoned buildings occupied by an enemy unit. The group encounters the unusual abilities of newly arrived kharakternyky and clears the identified enemy positions.

When an enemy drone attacks the group, Vedmid escapes through a portal with Phoenix and a prisoner to an orbital base. There he witnesses technology and capabilities that fundamentally change his understanding of the world.

KEY EVENTS AND RELATIONSHIPS

Comrades and a new world

  • An experienced infantryman; the manuscript states that he has fought since the beginning of the war.
