ZERO PLAN · INTER-PLANAR TRAVEL · EXPLORATION

Isaac

A kharakternyk who guides transitions between plans, helps inhabitants of distant worlds and explores unknown parts of the world tree.

PROFILE

Inter-planar travel and exploration

Isaac is a kharakternyk of the Zero Plan. He organises transitions between worlds, prepares expedition members and guides them during exploration. Before a transition, Isaac carefully explains the capabilities and limitations of the suits, points out potential risks and reminds the team of the main rule: patience and caution, even when everything seems familiar.

Status: Kharakternyk of the Zero Plan

Specialisation: Inter-planar travel and world exploration

Appearance: Blond hair, blue eyes and light stubble, following the approved character design

Age: Not specified in the reviewed manuscripts

JOE · SUPPORT AND EVACUATION

Almost two centuries of friendship

Isaac has known Joe for almost two hundred years. During that time, he brought water, medicine and equipment to Joe’s planet, took sick inhabitants to medical facilities for treatment, and then returned them home. The Three-Day Law did not allow visitors to remain on another plan for longer: after that period, a person had to return to their home world.

After the Kharakternyky of the Zero and First Plans united, the ability to transfer people permanently between plans also became available to the Zero Plan. Isaac received permission to evacuate the inhabitants and returned to Joe not with water, but with the chance to leave for good. Together with Joe, he evacuated all the planet’s inhabitants — 103 people — to the resort planet.

EXPEDITIONS

Preparing and guiding transitions

Isaac briefs expedition members. He explains how the suits work, their capabilities and limitations, and the potential dangers. On one expedition, Dmytro, Nairin and Leila join him. It is Leila’s first transition; she is included as a medic who already knows Dmytro.

Isaac also organises safe exploration of plans that have become inaccessible or dangerous to visit normally. For this work, the team prepared a protected, airtight dome from which they can observe the environment without going outside.

EXPLORING DISTANT BRANCHES

The boundary of the Ninth Plan

While reviewing old expedition logs, Isaac notices that the route once used by the Kharakternyky to travel freely from the Eighth Plan to the Ninth, and then to the Tenth, no longer works. He leaves his ship near the planet of the Eighth Plan and checks the direction of the transition again. The Ninth Plan does not seem to have disappeared completely, but the space between the worlds no longer allows a portal to form.

When the Ninth Plan finally ceases to exist for the Kharakternyky, Isaac remains near the Eighth World to investigate what happened. He deploys automated satellites and orbital stations, measures the planet’s gravitational field, tracks the movement of its natural satellites and compares results using independent methods. The data show no familiar signs of geological destruction, but Isaac continues his observations without jumping to conclusions.

CHARACTER AND WORKING APPROACH

Attention to detail

Isaac does not rush to conclusions. He repeats measurements, checks sensors, changes methods and tries to disprove his own assumptions before accepting them. His conduct during expeditions demonstrates caution, patience and responsibility for the people he guides.
