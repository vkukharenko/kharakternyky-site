Hetman Mykolai

Grandfather Mykola, who becomes the hetman of his world after its kharakternyk community begins to revive, and one of the leaders guiding three human civilisations towards an alliance.

KEY INFORMATION

Hetman of Earth-1

Mykolai, known to those close to him as Grandfather Mykola, is a kharakternyk and the hetman of a world that regained a connection to other Plans after Dmytro appeared. Before that, he was the last kharakternyk of his Plan. The arrival of new kharakternyky and his appointment as hetman marked the revival of the community.

Name: Mykolai

Also known as: Grandfather Mykola

Title: Hetman

World: First Plan / Earth-1

Status: Kharakternyk

Age: Not specified in the manuscripts

Appearance: An elderly man with long white hair and a thick white beard; the approved visual design shows him in an embroidered shirt and a dark leather vest

CHARACTER AND LEADERSHIP

Hospitality and firm decisions

Mykolai combines the warmth of a hospitable host with the authority of a leader. He welcomes newcomers and scientists into his home and listens carefully, but acts decisively when the safety and future of his civilisation are at stake.

In negotiations, Mykolai speaks directly, weighs each side’s strengths and looks for common ground. He does not pretend to understand everything, but is ready to take responsibility and propose a concrete course of action.

ABILITIES AND WORK

Kharakternyk and coordinator

As a kharakternyk, Mykolai uses portals and the thought-link. He can open transitions between Plans, maintain a group channel and transmit large thought-packets. He also works with maps of worlds and adds information preserved by his community to the shared holographic map.

As hetman, he coordinates the revival of the kharakternyky of his Plan, receives newly initiated people for training, brings in scientists and takes part in joint defence planning. Within the agreed plan, he assigns the civilian direction to Dmytro, while military coordination is handled by the responsible commanders, including Marat.

ROLE IN THE STORY

Reviving the community and uniting three worlds

Before Dmytro appeared, Mykolai was the last kharakternyk in his world. Together with his wife, a seer, he had been preparing for the inevitable end of their civilisation. The new connection between Plans changed that course: the community began to revive, and Mykolai became hetman after the position had remained vacant for more than a century.

At his meeting with Sukhum, he presents the map preserved by his Plan’s travellers and helps establish the links between the three central worlds. Later, Mykolai takes part in negotiations with Raul, formulates the proposal for an alliance and helps distribute responsibility among the leaders of the three civilisations.

Key events
  • Becomes the new hetman after Dmytro appears; the position had been vacant for more than a century.
  • Welcomes newly initiated kharakternyky at his home and arranges their training and placement for further study.
  • At his first meeting with Sukhum, presents the map of Plans known to his community and helps clarify the connections between Earth-0, Earth-1 and Earth-2.
  • Hosts groups of scientists brought to his Plan and helps provide space for their work.
  • After negotiations with Raul, sends the agreements to Dmytro and Marat and assigns their areas of responsibility.
  • Works with Sukhum and Raul on a joint action plan for the three civilisations.
  • Warns of the Order’s threat and takes part in defining security and secrecy measures.
Relationships
  • Hanna: Mykolai’s wife; the manuscripts identify her as a seer.
  • Dmytro: the first newly initiated kharakternyk, whose arrival opened a new connection between worlds; Mykolai entrusts him with the civilian direction of their joint work.
  • Hetman Sukhum: an ally and partner in coordinating the three worlds; mutual respect develops quickly between them.
  • Marat: a partner in military coordination and planning joint actions.
  • Raul: leader of the Second Plan, with whom Mykolai negotiates an alliance and joint defence.
Visual canon

An elderly man with long white hair and a thick white beard. The author-approved design shows him wearing a light embroidered shirt, a dark leather vest, dark trousers and boots. His exact age is not specified in the manuscripts.
