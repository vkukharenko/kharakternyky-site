NORTHERN STATE · MILITARY STORYLINE

Sardan

A serviceman of the Northern State. He received a thought-link bracelet and tried to cross the front line with several fellow soldiers to surrender to Ukrainian forces.

PROFILE

A soldier trying to survive

Sardan serves in a military unit of the Northern State. After receiving a thought-link bracelet, he learned that its users could communicate directly. He and several fellow soldiers decided to cross the front line and surrender to Ukrainian forces.

CHARACTER AND ACTIONS

Talkativeness that became dangerous

Sardan likes to talk, share news and boast that he knows more than others. Unable to keep the secret, he told outsiders about the bracelet and the planned escape, exposing the attempt.

ROLE IN THE STORY

The escape attempt and its consequences

His story shows how one person’s carelessness can put the other participants in a shared plan at risk.
