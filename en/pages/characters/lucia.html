MEXICO · MOLFAR'S STUDENTS · KHARAKTERNYKY

Lucía

A 17-year-old student of Molfar from Mexico. Lively, open and curious, she quickly connects with other people.

PROFILE

Energy, curiosity and openness

Lucía is one of Molfar's seven students who travel with him and Alisia to meet kharakternyk communities and develop their own abilities. She comes from Mexico. Lively, expressive and open in conversation, she easily connects with people and quickly becomes interested in new experiences.

Origin: Mexico

Age: 17 (according to the supplied visual reference)

Height: 168 cm (according to the supplied visual reference)

Build: Athletic and flexible

Hair: Thick, wavy, dark chestnut

Status: Initiated (according to the supplied visual reference)

Ability: Thought-link

ROLE AND PERSONALITY

A sociable and curious student

Lucía dislikes boredom and rules without explanations. She is devoted to her friends, brave and easy to talk to. Her curiosity shows in her desire to understand unfamiliar places and people: during the journey she asks questions, jokes and readily shares her impressions.

TRAINING

Thought-link and journeys

During their shared training, Lucía and the other students begin to experience the thought-link. She learns to transmit thoughts, images and short messages, while also learning to control whom she addresses and what she is willing to receive from others.

In group exercises, Lucía quickly becomes enthusiastic about this new ability and celebrates the first successes. She also takes part in journeys to kharakternyk communities, where she encounters their traditions and experience.
