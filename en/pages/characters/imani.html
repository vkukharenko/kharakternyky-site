PROFILE

A Kenyan student of Molfar

Imani is one of Molfar's seven most capable students. The story presents her as confident, decisive and fiery, particularly when someone tries to decide things on her behalf. She is curious about the new abilities of characterники and openly interested in learning portals.

Country: Kenya · Age: 17 (visual reference) · Height: 173 cm (visual reference)

Build: athletic, strong and flexible · Eyes: dark brown · Hair: long black afro-textured braids with beads · Dominant hand: right

CHARACTER

Determined and independent

In the story, Imani is confident, decisive and fiery. She dislikes others making decisions for her, asks direct questions and readily engages with new activities. When the group discusses learning independently, she points out that they could develop their abilities on their own.

The approved visual reference adds an energetic, emotional and competitive side: she values honesty and teamwork, enjoys challenges and makes decisions quickly. These are visual character-pattern notes; the story itself most clearly confirms her confidence, determination and fiery temperament.

TRAINING AND ABILITIES

Thought-link and portals

Imani is one of four newly initiated students—alongside Eero, Lucia and Haruto—learning to open portals. Molfar helps them sense the ability through a connection with Alicia. Their first successes are followed by fatigue, mistakes and many questions.

The thought-link lets them communicate without speaking aloud. During a conversation in a café, Imani jokes that they can now check Nicholas's thoughts. She is learning to use the link while respecting the boundaries of another person's mind.

Imani belongs to Molfar's group of seven most capable students, who are skilled with sabres and eager to improve.
