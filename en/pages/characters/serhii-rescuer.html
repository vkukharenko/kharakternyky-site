OVERVIEW

Serhii — Rescuer

Foreman of the United Humanity rescue team. He leads a search-and-rescue unit and takes part in complex evacuation operations.

Status: foreman of the United Humanity rescue team

Main activity: search and rescue, evacuation

Team: rescuers, search specialists, medics and technical personnel

Age: not specified in the reviewed passages

Abilities: no supernatural abilities are confirmed in the rescue-operation scenes

Appearance: follows the author's approved visual design

SEARCH AND RESCUE

Leading the operation on site

During the response to attacks, Serhii directs the team's deployment, gives orders and checks information received from residents. He will not allow rescuers to work beneath an unstable slab without shoring, and when another explosion strikes nearby, he orders everyone away from the building.

When a voice or tapping is heard beneath the rubble, Serhii stops the engines and asks everyone to remain silent so they can determine the direction. The team uses search drones, cameras, jacks, hydraulic tools and structural supports.

ATTACKS · EVACUATION

Decisions under pressure

During repeated attacks, Serhii must weigh the danger to rescuers against the need to continue searching for people trapped beneath the debris. He moves part of the team to safety while keeping the specialists needed to continue the operation at the site.

Working with Olha, he quickly adapts the team to the use of portals: rescuers can reach inaccessible voids, equipment can be delivered directly to the work area, and casualties can be transferred to medics much faster. Serhii himself continues entering collapsed structures alongside the rescuers.

OLHA · FORMER TEAM

Working together again

Serhii knows Olha from her previous work with the rescue team. When she appears at the scene of another attack, he is surprised to see her, but does not waste time asking questions: people remain trapped beneath the rubble.

Later, Serhii asks whether Olha wants to return. She tells him that she has already returned. They continue working side by side, and Serhii supports her during difficult shifts, including bringing her water after a long operation.

CHARACTER AND STORY ROLE

Responsibility for people

In the available scenes, Serhii is a composed leader who maintains order during attacks and does not expose his team to unnecessary danger. He pays attention to signals from survivors, persists with the search and keeps working while there is still a chance to save someone.

His story role is connected with the rescue service and Olha's return to her former team. His age, family, origins and personal history are not established in the reviewed passages.
