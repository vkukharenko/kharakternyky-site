Varg

A Sarg mercenary and master swordsman. An elite fighter, mentor and sparring partner.

KEY INFORMATION

A master swordsman among the Sargs

Varg is a Sarg mercenary known as the best swordsman in his squad. He serves as an elite fighter, mentor and sparring partner, passing on combat experience and testing the skills of others in duels.

Name: Varg

Species: Sarg

Status: Mercenary

Specialisation: Master swordsman

Role: Elite fighter, mentor, sparring partner

Height: Approximately 1.3 times a human's height

Age: Not specified in the available material

APPEARANCE AND EQUIPMENT

Scaled skin and armour

Varg has a massive build and dark, scaled skin with a pronounced stone-like texture. His face is distinguished by deep-set red eyes, a powerful jaw and long tendril-like locks. He wears dark layered gear with metal details and forearm protection.

His weapon is secured behind his shoulder. He is not shown holding it in his hands.

PERSONALITY

A personal code and respect for skill

Varg is proud, straightforward and hot-tempered. He respects strength and skill, remains loyal to his own code and values an honest fight. He treats worthy opponents with respect and remembers unfinished duels.

COMBAT SKILLS

The squad’s best swordsman

His main specialisation is swordsmanship. As a mentor and sparring partner, Varg helps other fighters refine their skills and test themselves in combat.

His age, origins, family ties and detailed story events have not yet been established in the available material, so they are not filled in by assumption.
