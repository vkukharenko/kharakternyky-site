General Bakir

A military general of the Second Plan. He coordinates air forces and contributes to strategic defence and evacuation planning.

PROFILE

Commander of the Second Plan

Bakir is a general of the Second Plan involved in coordinating military operations and the large-scale evacuation of civilians. In combat scenes, he monitors the air situation, directs fighter deployments and moves reserves to threatened sectors. In strategic meetings, he works with transfer-flow calculations and resource planning.

Age: 44

Height: 188 cm

Status: General of the Second Plan

Position: Military general

Specialisation: Strategic planning, operational command, inter-plan security

ROLE IN THE STORY

Defence and evacuation

During the fighting, Bakir commands the Second Plan's fighter forces, responds to enemy aircraft concentrating near the city-path, and redirects reserves to sectors where the enemy is trying to gain an advantage. During the civilian evacuation, he oversees transport hubs and works with other leaders to plan permissible transfer flows between plans.

In later discussions, he contributes to decisions about relocating housing-module production and allocating the resources needed to save the population.

APPEARANCE

According to the supplied visual profile: 44 years old, 188 cm tall, short dark-blond hair, blue eyes and short stubble. He wears the Second Plan's dark tactical uniform with gold insignia and equipment.
