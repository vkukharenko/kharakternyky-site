Nairin

A kharakternyk who can perceive more in a person's thought-field than emotions and isolated images.

PROFILE

A rare ability to read the thought-field

Nairin is a kharakternyk of the Zero Plan. He is the only kharakternyk of that Plan who can fully read another person's thought-field. He does more than sense emotions or isolated images: he can perceive connections between thoughts, hidden reactions, inner contradictions and a person's true motives.

The ability rarely brings him comfort. Over the years, Nairin has learned to assess not only what people say, but also what they try to conceal — sometimes even from themselves.

Name: Nairin

World: Zero Plan

Status: kharakternyk

Special ability: full reading of a thought-field

Family: younger brother Zairin

Story role: helps Dmytro learn to use the thought-link

PAST AND SEPARATION

Twenty years of searching

Nairin had barely turned twelve when an aviation accident took both of his parents. From then on, he effectively became responsible for his younger brother Zairin: looking after their home, his brother's education and supporting him after the loss of their family.

Later, the brothers were separated. Nairin spent ten years in a village far from home, five years alongside the Major, lived through the Great Loss, and then spent another five years travelling between worlds. Each inhabited world left him with the hope that Zairin might be somewhere nearby.

RETURN

Reunion with his brother

After a long separation, Nairin hears Zairin's voice through the thought-link. At first, he can barely answer: the realisation that his brother is alive is stronger than words. Zairin asks him to stay where he is, afraid of losing his brother again.

The meeting brings the twenty-year period of separation and searching to an end.

TRAINING DMYTRO

Learning to use the thought-link

After Dmytro's initiation, Nairin helps him settle into the station and explains what the thought-link can do. During a walk, he gives Dmytro short practical exercises: isolate a signal, send a brief message and locate a specific person.

Nairin is reserved and wary in conversation. He watches strangers closely, notices inconsistencies in their behaviour and does not rush to trust their words.
