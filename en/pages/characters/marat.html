Marat

A First Plan kharakternyk who went from running a small Kyiv café to becoming a chorunzhyi ataman.

PROFILE

From a café to the kharakternyk community

Before his initiation, Marat was an ordinary Ukrainian from Kyiv and the owner of a small café near the Kyiv Pechersk Lavra. He knew coffee exceptionally well, cared about his customers and loved his work. His family has Caucasian roots, but Marat considers himself Ukrainian. Hetman Mykolai explains that the ability to become a kharakternyk does not depend on genetic ancestry.

After meeting Dmytro, Dido Mykolai and Nairin, Marat agrees to train on Nairin’s plan. He quickly learns to communicate through the thought-link and soon initiates his nephew. The nephew’s new connection reaches friends and family, and by the following morning a large group of newly connected people has gathered near the café.

Origin: Kyiv, Ukraine

Family roots: Caucasus

Former occupation: café owner and barista

Status: First Plan kharakternyk

Rank: Chorunzhyi ataman of the First Plan

Weapons: two combat sabres

Skills: thought-link communication, portals and sabre combat

TRAINING AND INITIATION

Rapidly mastering the unfamiliar

After receiving his first instructions, Marat begins training. In a short time he becomes confident using the thought-link and helps extend initiation to people close to him. His nephew, two friends and a female friend are among the first to respond to the new connection; the process then spreads through their families and acquaintances.

After nine days of training, Marat faces a trial to earn his own sabres. In a fight against Dmytro and Nairin, he demonstrates quick reactions, agility, endurance and the ability to adapt his tactics. He wins the weapons and continues the fight with sabres.

CHORUNZHYI ATAMAN

Skill and responsibility

After the trial, Hetman Mykolai offers Marat not only the sabres but also the rank of chorunzhyi ataman of the First Plan. Marat initially doubts whether he is ready for the responsibility, but ultimately accepts the appointment. Dmytro and Nairin congratulate him as an equal.

In later events, Marat takes part in meetings of the kharakternyk leaders and works on the military direction. His story brings together care for people, love of his craft and the determination he shows in combat.

PERSONALITY

Thoughtful in everyday life, decisive under pressure

Marat pays attention to details and to the people around him. His passion for coffee reflects patience, observation and a desire to master his craft. When challenged, he acts quickly, persists and does not give up easily, while learning to accept responsibility beyond himself.
