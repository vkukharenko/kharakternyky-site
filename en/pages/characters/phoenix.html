FIRST PLAN · MILITARY STORYLINE

Phoenix

A Ukrainian soldier facing the trials of war alongside his comrades.

PROFILE

A soldier on the front line

Phoenix is a Ukrainian soldier whose storyline presents the war from an infantryman's perspective. He operates as part of defensive and reconnaissance-assault teams, takes part in the defence of settlements, the capture of enemy soldiers and engagements with combat robots.

Name / callsign: Phoenix

Affiliation: Ukrainian defence forces

Role: serviceman, assault-team member

Age: not specified in the manuscripts

Appearance: not described in detail in the text; the supplied photograph is the visual reference

Equipment: tactical armour, helmet display, thought-link, needle launcher

CHARACTER AND CONDUCT

Decisiveness and responsibility for his team

Phoenix assesses situations carefully, keeps track of his comrades and does not rush to use lethal force when an objective can be achieved by taking prisoners. He can act quickly under fire while retaining a critical view of what is happening around him.

The arrival of unusual newcomers initially makes him distrustful. He gradually recognises their effectiveness, even though their methods and decisions sometimes make him reconsider his own place in the team.

ABILITIES AND SKILLS

Training and combat equipment

Phoenix uses the thought-link to exchange tactical information with other defenders, receives data through tactical displays and coordinates with ground reconnaissance drones. In engagements with robots, he uses a needle launcher, changing its firing mode and targeting vulnerable structural components.

The thought-link is a communication system for the defenders, not a voice channel for narrating every command: participants share the situation and positions through direct mental exchange.

EVENTS AND RELATIONSHIPS

Combat record

  • Vedmid (Bear) is Phoenix's comrade. They work together, cover each other's positions and conduct operations to capture enemy personnel.
  • Sokil (Falcon) commands the team during its mission in the industrial zone and the engagement with combat robots.
  • Ihor, Stas and Pol are three unusual newcomers assigned to the team. Their skills significantly affect the course of its operations.
  • During the defence of a settlement, Phoenix and Vedmid hold their position and engage the enemy as it approaches the buildings.
  • During a prisoner-capture operation, he tries to spare the lives of enemy soldiers who surrender.
  • In the industrial zone, he takes part in a battle against combat robots and helps identify and target their vulnerable structural components.
Information not established by the manuscripts

Phoenix's real name, age, height, birthplace, family and pre-war biography are not established in the reviewed texts. These details have deliberately been left out.
