UNDERGROUND CITIES · BOOKS ONE AND TWO

Vlad

Artem and Denys's friend. Together with them, he discovers the underground city, gets to know Kair and gradually adjusts to life after the relocation.

KEY INFORMATION

Artem and Denys's friend

Vlad is one of Artem's closest friends. At the beginning of the story, he waits for Artem near the house with Denys, follows the news about the portals and shares videos he has found. After moving to the underground city, Vlad does not withdraw into the new environment: he suggests exploring it, is interested in the possibility of going to the surface and joins the group's outings.

When talking to Kair, Vlad quickly moves from caution to relaxed, friendly interaction. He joins in the jokes, asks direct questions and helps his new acquaintance feel at ease among the teenagers.

ROLE IN THE STORY

Exploring the new world with his friends

At the beginning, Vlad brings his friends a video of a portal, and together they try to understand what is happening. When the conversation turns to the bracelets, he suggests that they may have been issued for more than simply granting access to protected complexes.

In the underground city, Vlad takes part in the group's everyday adventures: he suggests going to the surface, joins explorations of new sectors and supports the idea of travelling to another underground city. In these scenes, he is an active member of the group who likes to test new possibilities in practice.

His friendship with Artem and Denys gradually extends to Kair as well. For Vlad, his new friend's origin in another Plan does not prevent ordinary teenage friendship.

CHARACTER AND BEHAVIOUR

Open to new experiences

The story scenes portray Vlad as sociable and involved in shared activities. He shares information, suggests ideas for outings and trips, responds to his friends' jokes and is not afraid to ask questions. His character is revealed mainly through dialogue and everyday decisions rather than direct authorial description.

In the visual reference, his appearance is complemented by an attentive, sometimes thoughtful gaze and a broad smile. These traits belong to the approved visual design and are not separate biographical facts.
