Avhur

A military analyst in the Armed Forces of Ukraine on the Zero Plan.

PROFILE

An intelligence network analyst

Avhur serves as a military analyst in the Armed Forces of Ukraine on the Zero Plan. He leads the development of an intelligence network combining data from orbital modules, ground observers and analytical teams.

INTELLIGENCE NETWORK

From observation to verified data

Under his direction, the network covers important areas of the Northern State, builds a database of verified sites, detects anomalies and continuously updates its information.

ROLE IN MILITARY OPERATIONS

Finding and verifying targets

Avhur organises the work so the system does more than flag suspicious sites: it helps direct reconnaissance teams to verify them in person.
