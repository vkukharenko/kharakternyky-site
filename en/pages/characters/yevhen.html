Yevhen

An entrepreneur and systems organiser who helps turn the kharakternyky community into a society capable not only of surviving, but of flourishing.

PROFILE

From benefactor to organiser of a new system

Yevhen is a wealthy Ukrainian entrepreneur whom Grandpa Mykola brings into a long-term partnership. At first, he regards the visitors with caution and evaluates them carefully. After seeing the capabilities of the Zero Plan, he agrees to support scientific and medical projects. His role soon goes far beyond funding: he builds teams, coordinates resources and helps make decisions that affect people's safety and future.

Role: entrepreneur, organiser and negotiator

Fields: economics, finance, science, industry and civil infrastructure

Status: member of the community; holder of a thought-link bracelet

Abilities: uses thought-link through the bracelet; personal initiation or supernatural abilities are not confirmed in the text

Age and height: not established in the manuscripts

Appearance: the supplied portrait is used as the visual reference, not as a substitute for a textual description

Key connections: Grandpa Mykola, Lech, Vasyl, Zaib, Ukrainian officials and industrial teams

Appearances: all three supplied books

CHARACTER AND CONVICTIONS

Humanity must do more than survive

Yevhen thinks on a large scale and in systems. He understands that a new society cannot consist only of kharakternyky: it also needs engineers, production managers, economists, scientists and people who can organise complex systems. He proposes bringing people without kharakternyk abilities into the shared effort and says he wants to build a common future, not a separate society for the gifted.

He relies on personal conversations and his ability to assess people. When building his team, he rules out candidates he considers too harsh, greedy or accustomed to power. He looks instead for capable people who can build complex systems and remain decent. He does not force the thought-link bracelet on anyone; he offers it to those willing to join.

SCIENCE, MEDICINE AND TEAM BUILDING

Building the foundations for cooperation

After reaching an agreement with Grandpa Mykola, Yevhen provides premises for a scientific centre, including a laboratory complex in the capital and additional space for researchers. He supports the medical programme and brings doctors from his own clinics into the work. Scientists gain access to research materials and technologies from the Zero Plan.

Within days, Yevhen assembles an initial team of business leaders, doctors, scientists, engineers, administrators, logistics specialists and finance professionals. His work helps turn the community's rapid, unstructured growth into an organisation capable of coordinating large projects.

EVACUATION AND PROTECTION

From underground cities to a network of shelters

Yevhen helps organise relocation to underground cities. His team works with local administrations and schools, holds public meetings, distributes bracelets, prepares entrances and coordinates the flow of residents. As attacks intensify, he supports the decision not to wait and to admit the entire population of Sumy into its underground city, even if this means temporarily increasing residential density.

Later, Yevhen works on the shelter deployment programme. Together with Zaib and government officials, he assesses the limits of separate modules and supports connecting them into a network of underground passages and hub complexes. These hubs are intended to include medical centres, supplies, independent power and dispatch facilities, allowing people to move between protected sites without going above ground.

ECONOMICS AND NEGOTIATIONS

Protecting Ukraine's capabilities

Yevhen helps shape the economic rules for cooperation between Ukraine and the Zero Plan. He explains that new technologies do not abolish the market; they change it. Quality, uniqueness, speed and the value of work still matter.

During negotiations with the Alliance over laser systems, he agrees to a technology transfer but insists on a phased process, funding for Ukrainian technical teams, guarantees and compensation. He does not want Ukraine to lose its own manufacturing capacity through a rushed transfer of the entire production cycle. In the third book, the president specifically notes that Yevhen skilfully keeps the Alliance focused on laser turrets while other developments are being prepared.

REFERENCE DETAILS

What the text confirms

Confirmed role and traits
  • A wealthy entrepreneur, explicitly called an oligarch in his first appearance.
  • A proactive organiser who sees the need for a systemic economy, science, industry and administration.
  • Skilled at selecting people, conducting complex negotiations and coordinating resources.
  • Works across scientific, medical, evacuation, infrastructure and economic programmes.
Thought-link and status

Yevhen receives a thought-link bracelet and uses it for information access, communication and coordination. The supplied texts do not confirm that he has undergone initiation or can open portals himself.

Appearance and unknown details

The manuscripts do not establish Yevhen's exact age, height or a detailed physical description. In his first scene, he is described as a short, slender, elderly but energetic man. The more detailed visual reference on this page is based on the supplied photograph.

Sources across the series

Book one: meeting Grandpa Mykola, becoming a benefactor, proposing to involve people without special abilities, building a team and a scientific centre, and taking on economic and organisational work. Book two: relocation to underground cities, economics, and negotiations over weapons and technology. Book three: completion of the shelter programme and planning a connected network of protected complexes.
