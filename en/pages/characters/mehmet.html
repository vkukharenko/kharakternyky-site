TURKEY · MILITARY STORYLINE

General Mehmet

A Turkish general who maintained a close professional and personal friendship with Ukrainian General Oleksii for years.

KEY INFORMATION

General of the Turkish army

Mehmet is a Turkish general who regularly exchanges operational information with Ukrainian General Oleksii on logistics, intelligence and weapons supplies. Over three years of cooperation, they became friends, and their regular calls became an opportunity not only to fulfil official duties but also to speak as friends.

ROLE IN THE STORY

Friend, ally and representative of the Order

Mehmet warns Oleksii about a planned betrayal and the reduction of support for Ukraine. Later, acting on behalf of the Order, he delivers an ultimatum demanding capitulation. His personal feelings for his longtime friend remain, but Mehmet acts as a representative of the organisation he joined.

Oleksii refuses the Order's terms. Mehmet conveys the answer, although accepting his friend's choice is difficult for him. After circumstances change, military cooperation between them resumes within official procedures.
