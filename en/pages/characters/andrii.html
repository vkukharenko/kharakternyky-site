FIRST PLAN · CIVILIAN EVACUATION

Andrii

A city resident who reaches a newly built protected shelter during a massive attack.

OVERVIEW

An ordinary person caught in a mass evacuation

Andrii lives in a city that comes under a massive attack. When explosions draw close to his building, he quickly takes his documents, leaves his apartment and heads with his neighbours to a newly built shelter. His route leads through the night-time bombardment, a stream of evacuating residents and several city blocks to the protected entrance.

SCENE · THE SHELTER

The way to safety

An explosion wakes Andrii. After further strikes, he realises that the city is under attack by strike drones and aerial munitions. He does not waste time collecting belongings: he takes his documents and leaves the apartment. In the corridor he meets a neighbour with her young daughter, and they go downstairs together.

Outside, Andrii sees residents hurrying towards the new shelter. People help one another: they lift those who have fallen, support the elderly and make sure children are not left alone. After another explosion, the crowd stops for a moment, then continues towards the entrance.

At the entrance, Andrii's bracelet, issued only a few days earlier, gives a short beep. Inside, he passes through a protected airlock and finds not an ordinary basement but a fully equipped complex with ventilation, lighting, medical assistance, water and food. Only then does he allow himself to breathe out and sit down.

CHARACTER AND STORY ROLE

Focus on what matters

During the evacuation, Andrii's thoughts narrow to simple tasks: reach the shelter, keep moving and do not fall behind. He does not try to understand every detail of the attack or waste time on belongings he can leave behind. His scene shows the experience of an ordinary resident during a massive strike and the operation of the new civil-protection system.

The reviewed passages do not show Andrii using kharakternyk abilities, nor do they establish his occupation or a separate storyline. These details are not added without manuscript support.
