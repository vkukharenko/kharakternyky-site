MILITARY AVIATION · AIR COMBAT

Marek

A fighter pilot captain. He leads his flight in the air battle near the city-path and later finds himself on another Plan after passing through a portal.

PROFILE

A pilot who trusts his aircraft and experience

Marek is a captain and a combat fighter pilot. During the air battle near the city-path, he leads his flight and operates alongside his wingman and other pilots. He tracks the aircraft positions, assesses threats and changes course to intercept enemy fighters and prevent them from reaching the ground forces.

Rank: Captain

Role: combat fighter pilot

Unit: flight

Responsibility: leads his flight in air combat

Story episodes: Day 17 — “Pilot. Air Breakthrough”; Day 18 — “Marek. Another Plan”

Age and height: not specified in the manuscript

DAY 17 · AIR BREAKTHROUGH

Combat operations above the resort planet

Marek passes through a portal as part of the first group of fighters. Once in the skies above the resort planet, he checks the instruments, engines, fuel supply and the position of his group. The aircraft hold formation while the pilots distribute targets and cover one another.

During the battle, Marek evades interceptors, changes altitude and course, launches against assigned targets and pulls his aircraft out of dangerous trajectories. He checks that all aircraft in his group remain in formation and leads them into the next attack run. He is an experienced pilot who knows his aircraft well and remains aware of the wider situation.

DAY 18 · ANOTHER PLAN

A portal after which the thrust disappears

The air battle spreads across the settlement. Marek leads his flight in the central sector. When an enemy fighter tries to break through toward the ground forces, Marek changes course and moves to its flank. At that moment, a portal opens directly in front of his aircraft. He tries to evade it, but cannot complete the manoeuvre in time and passes through.

In the unfamiliar sky, the fighter begins to lose thrust. Marek increases engine power, but there is no acceleration and the aircraft loses speed. The instruments show no critical malfunction, and the controls remain operational. He levels the aircraft, looks for a landing site and transitions into a glide. The landing on an empty plain is hard, but he brings the fighter to a stop and inspects the engine: there is no visible damage.

His radio call to the flight receives no answer. Later, another fighter emerges through a portal and also loses thrust. After speaking with the other pilot, Marek considers the atmosphere as a possible cause — its composition or density may prevent jet engines from producing the required thrust. This remains his hypothesis, not a confirmed explanation.

CHARACTER AND SKILLS

Experience, composure and a feel for flight

In combat, Marek is composed and decisive. He knows how his fighter behaves, understands the limits of a manoeuvre and can monitor his aircraft, his wingman and the wider air battle at the same time. Even after arriving on an unfamiliar Plan, he does not panic: he checks the instruments, keeps control of the aircraft and looks for a safe landing.

What the manuscript confirms
  • Marek holds the rank of captain.
  • He is a combat fighter pilot and leads his flight.
  • He takes part in air battles near the city-path.
  • After passing through a portal, his fighter loses thrust but remains controllable.
  • He glides and makes a forced landing on an empty plain.
  • The atmosphere affecting engine performance is the character's hypothesis, not a final explanation.
