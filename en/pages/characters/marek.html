SECOND PLAN · MILITARY AVIATION

Marek

A combat pilot. During the air battle near the city-path, he is caught by a portal trap and finds himself on a Plan where jet engines cannot operate.

PROFILE

A pilot beyond the familiar sky

Marek is a combat pilot taking part in the air battle near the city-path. He operates as part of a flight and is responsible for his own group of aircraft. Marek is not the commander of the air group or the overall operation: his responsibility is limited to his flight.

Role: combat pilot

Unit: flight

Responsibility: leads his own flight

Storyline: Day 17 · “The Trap” / “Another Plan”

Age and height: not established in confirmed canon

Special abilities: not confirmed

AIR BATTLE

The portal trap

During the battle, the Second Plan's fighters engage the attackers in an aerial interception. The attackers are forced to focus on the air battle and can no longer act effectively on the ground. A portal suddenly opens in front of Marek. He tries to evade it, but there is not enough time.

After crossing over, his aircraft loses thrust. Marek retains control of the machine and uses its remaining speed to glide before making a hard landing. Around him, he sees other aircraft and pilots who have found themselves in the same situation.

ANOTHER PLAN

A world where engines fall silent

On this Plan, the atmosphere is extremely dense, and its composition interferes with jet-engine operation. Conventional aircraft cannot fly under engine thrust, but remain controllable and can glide. Marek does not yet know why the failure occurs; he only has his immediate experience of the emergency and sees that other pilots have encountered the same problem.

Limits of confirmed canon

The confirmed facts are Marek's role as a combat pilot, command of his own flight only, sudden passage through a portal, loss of thrust while retaining control, gliding and forced landing. His age, height, family, origin and later fate are not established in these scenes.
