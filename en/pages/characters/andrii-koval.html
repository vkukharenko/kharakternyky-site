FIRST PLAN · SECURITY SERVICE OF UKRAINE

General Andrii Ivanovych Koval

Deputy Head of the Security Service of Ukraine. A restrained leader accustomed to assessing people and events without unnecessary noise.

PROFILE

Experience, composure and attention to detail

Andrii Ivanovych Koval is a general and Deputy Head of the Security Service of Ukraine. He belongs to the group of senior officials dealing with national security and the coordination of complex operations.

Full name: Andrii Ivanovych Koval

Rank: General

Position: Deputy Head of the SSU

Age: around 60

Height: around 180 cm

Build: strong, athletic

Appearance: short grey hair, grey eyes

Character: calm, attentive, demanding, experienced and strategic-minded

CHARACTER AND PRINCIPLES

Few words, close attention

Koval does not seek attention through loud statements. He is focused, restrained and demanding, and values professionalism and results. His authority comes from experience, attentiveness and the ability to see the wider picture.

“What matters is seeing the substance, not the noise.”

ROLE IN THE STORY

A representative of the country's security service

In his official capacity, Koval takes part in discussions of national security, risk assessment and coordination. Specific scenes and decisions will be added in line with the manuscript rather than attributing unconfirmed events to him.

Visual canon

The main image combines two author-approved depictions of the same person. Koval wears a restrained SSU uniform with Ukrainian insignia; he has short grey hair, grey eyes and a strong build.
