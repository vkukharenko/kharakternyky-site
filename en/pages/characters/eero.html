CHARACTERS · FINLAND

Eero

A Finnish student of Molfar who discovers the possibilities of the thought-link and portals.

PROFILE

A Finnish student of Molfar

Eero is one of Molfar's seven most capable students. The story describes him as a tall, quiet Finnish young man who prefers to assess a situation before acting. Alongside the other students, he trains with the sabre and works to improve his skills.

Country: Finland · Age: 17 (visual reference) · Height: 189 cm (visual reference)

Status: newly initiated characterник · Abilities: thought-link; learning to open portals.

CHARACTER

Observes before acting

Eero is reserved and attentive. He does not rush to conclusions and asks questions that help him understand unfamiliar phenomena. He takes part in the group's training without seeking the spotlight.

TRAINING AND ABILITIES

Thought-link and portals

After initiation, Eero joins Lucia, Haruto and Imani in developing the thought-link. In one scene, Haruto hears Eero's thought even though Eero has not spoken aloud. The students later practise deliberately transmitting clear images to one another.

Eero is also one of four newly initiated students learning to open portals. Molfar helps them experience the skill through a connection with Alicia. They make mistakes, tire, and gradually learn to control the transitions.

All seven of Molfar's students are skilled with sabres and eager to improve.
