THE DARK ONE'S ARMY · MILITARY STORYLINE

Sael

A soldier in the Dark One's army: disciplined, cold-minded and utterly devoted to his master.

PROFILE

A soldier who never treats his duty lightly

Sael serves in the Dark One's army. He is not an armoured-unit commander but an ordinary soldier. He is defined by strict discipline, cold calculation and unquestioning loyalty to the Dark One.

Age: 20

Height: 188 cm

Affiliation: the Dark One's army

Status: soldier, not a commander

Character: cold, stern, disciplined and merciless

Skills: experienced military operative with a strong grasp of tactics

CHARACTER AND CONDUCT

Self-control and absolute loyalty

Sael does not hesitate easily and does not let emotion interfere with carrying out an assignment. He thinks tactically, assesses circumstances carefully and acts harshly when the situation demands it.

His training and discipline make him a dangerous opponent, but they do not change his place in the hierarchy: Sael remains a soldier, not a unit commander.

VISUAL CANON

Appearance and equipment

A young man with tousled dark-brown hair, blue-green eyes and short stubble. He wears dark tactical gear bearing the insignia of the Dark One's army.

Canon note

The label “armoured-unit commander” on an earlier visual mock-up is incorrect and is not part of the character's canon.
