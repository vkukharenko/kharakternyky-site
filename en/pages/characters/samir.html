LEBANON · MOLFAR'S STUDENTS · KHARAKTERNYKY

Samir

A 17-year-old student of Molfar from Lebanon. Quiet and observant, he listens more than he speaks and dislikes being rushed.

PROFILE

Observation and quiet focus

Samir is one of Molfar's seven most capable students who travel with him and Alisia. He comes from Lebanon. Unlike Nikolas, Samir does not try to fill every silence with conversation: he listens carefully, observes people and circumstances, and takes his time before reaching conclusions.

Origin: Lebanon

Age: 17

Height: 177 cm

Build: Lean and enduring

Eyes: Dark brown

Hair: Thick, wavy black

Dominant hand: Right

Status: Not initiated

Potential: High

ROLE AND PERSONALITY

The one who listens first

Samir is calm and even-tempered, attentive to others and careful with his words. He dislikes being rushed, supports his friends and rarely displays strong emotions. His reserve does not mean indifference: he feels things deeply but does not always show it.

His visual design includes practical clothing for long journeys, a keffiyeh with a traditional Middle Eastern pattern, bracelets and a personal amulet — the Hand of Fatima, a symbol of protection and balance. He has a small old scar on his face and an earring in his left ear.

FIRST MANIFESTATION

Thought-link

During the students' meeting in Cherkasy, Samir unexpectedly catches another person's thought — a mention of the sea. It happens during the students' first shared manifestation of the thought-link. Samir does not seek attention and reacts quietly, but the episode confirms that he too can perceive other people's thoughts.

Further training is meant to help him understand this new ability, learn to transmit thoughts deliberately and control what he receives from others.
