KHARAKTERNYKY · MEXICO · SIERRA HOUSE

Esteban

A trusted man of the Patron, coordinator and guide of the Sierra House. Initiated in Ukraine, where he fought on the Ukrainian side.

PROFILE

A man of the Sierra House

Esteban is Mexican and belongs to the Sierra House. He welcomes visitors, helps them settle in and accompanies them during their stay at the House. He is trusted by the Patron and understands the changes among the Mexican Houses following the emergence of the new kharakternyky.

Origin: Mexico, Sierra House

Age: 35 (visual reference)

Status: kharakternyk, initiated in Ukraine

Role: trusted aide to the Patron, coordinator and guide

Abilities: thought-link and short-range portals

Allegiance: remains a member of his House and respects the Patron's authority

BACKGROUND

The war in Ukraine and his initiation

At the beginning of the full-scale war, members of several Mexican groups began arriving in Ukraine in separate teams. Some called themselves mercenaries. Their leaders wanted real combat experience: learning to operate in modern warfare under the constant threat of drones and artillery, and gaining skills in reconnaissance and unit coordination.

Esteban was among those who chose to remain in Ukraine as long as he could be useful. He initially fought as an ordinary combatant. While in Ukraine, he underwent a spontaneous initiation. He had not planned to become a kharakternyk and did not immediately understand what was happening to him.

After the initiation, he discovered the ability to open short portals between two points on the same Plan. He later learned to control these transitions and understand the limits of his abilities. He also joined the shared thought-space of the newly initiated, regardless of their origins or former House affiliations.

RELATIONSHIPS AND POSITION

Loyalty to his House

After returning to the Sierra House, Esteban did not abandon his origins or his allegiance to the Patron. He respects the Patron's authority while maintaining his connection with the wider community of new kharakternyky.

Esteban explains that people initiated in Ukraine, despite belonging to different Mexican Houses, can now communicate continuously, exchange information and coordinate their affairs. This has not dissolved the Houses' independence or created a single governing body: each Patron retains authority over their own House.

He misses the comrades with whom he fought in Ukraine; not all of them returned. At the same time, he believes he can now help his House not only with weapons and military experience, but also through his new abilities and connections.

CHARACTER PATTERN

Visual reference

Appearance and clothing

35 years old. Dark curly hair, moustache and short beard. Practical light-coloured clothing, leather straps and bracelets; a tattoo on his arm. A medallion hangs from a necklace. These details come from the approved visual reference, not the narrative text.

Canon and sources

Story details were checked against the third supplied book, especially the passage “Alicia-02. The Sierra House”. Age, appearance, clothing, medallion and tattoo are recorded from the visual reference. The book does not specify Esteban's exact age.
