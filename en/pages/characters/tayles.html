LORDS' COUNCIL · MISSILE FORCES

Lord Tayles

Commander of his world's missile forces.

PROFILE

Missile forces

Lord Tayles is responsible for the missile units and large ammunition reserves of his world. During the assault on the city-path, he organizes a sustained missile barrage intended to exhaust its air defences rather than destroy the structure with a single strike.

Title: Lord

Position: Commander of the missile forces

Reports to: The Supreme

Coordinates with: Lord Olaf and other commanders

DAY 13 · THE LORDS' COUNCIL

Remembering the destroyed holdings

The Supreme reminds Tayles how the Zero Ones' forces destroyed his holdings and how his people pleaded for mercy. Two or three settlements were lost. Tayles does not look away; pain and anger are visible in his eyes, but he does not deny what happened.

DAY 15 · MISSILE STRIKE

Exhausting the defences

Tayles oversees hours of missile launches through portals, attacking the city-path from several directions. Under the Supreme's plan, his task is to force the air defences to expend their interceptors. The city-path's outer shell cannot be breached by an ordinary massed strike: it is designed to withstand even an indirect nuclear blast nearby.

Tayles has access to vast missile reserves that had remained unused for years.

DAY 18 · ATTEMPT TO RESUME THE MISSILE STRIKE

Portal limitations

When the available dark characterники become scarce, Tayles tries to resume missile launches with those who remain available. The ammunition and launch crews are still in place, but the main constraint is now the shortage of characterники able to open portals beside the missile launchers.
