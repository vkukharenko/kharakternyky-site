CANONICAL APPEARANCE

LORD TAYLES

Pain and anger

Tayles is a dark-haired member of the Council of Lords. When the Supreme reminds him of the old war, he speaks directly and meets his gaze; pain and anger over the deaths in his domain are evident in his expression.

Status: Council lord.

Appearance: Dark hair.

Personal loss: Two or three of his settlements were destroyed during the old war.

COUNCIL OF LORDS

Memory of destroyed settlements

The Supreme reminds Tayles how his people begged for mercy during an attack by the Zero-worlders. The recollection immediately changes the tone of the conversation: Tayles does not deny the past or minimise the losses.
