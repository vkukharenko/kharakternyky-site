Yaroslav

Dmytro's deputy, who begins learning to use his kharakternyk abilities after his initiation.

PROFILE

Dmytro's deputy

Before discovering the world of the kharakternyky, Yaroslav worked on Dmytro's team and served as his deputy. The manuscript describes him as tall and broad-shouldered, with dark hair tied in a short ponytail and an alert, slightly predatory gaze.

Yaroslav trusted Dmytro and agreed to go with him without hesitation. After learning about the kharakternyky, he became a newly initiated member — confused and excited, still untrained, but full of potential.

Name: Yaroslav

Role before initiation: Dmytro's deputy

Status: newly initiated kharakternyk

Training: at the beginning; not yet trained

Appearance: tall and broad-shouldered; dark hair tied in a short ponytail

Notable trait: considerable potential yet to be developed

INITIATION

Trust in Dmytro

When Dmytro returns to the office, he tells Yaroslav about the new world and shows him his latest work. During their conversation, Dmytro tests whether Yaroslav can perceive mental communication and realises that he, too, is one of the kharakternyky.

At first, Yaroslav does not notice the unusual way they are communicating. When Dmytro explains that he has been speaking to him telepathically, Yaroslav reacts with surprise and disbelief. Dmytro gradually shares the information he needs and suggests checking the other members of the team.

SEARCH IN EUROPE

First independent assignments

After his initial training, Yaroslav joins the search for latent kharakternyky. He works in Europe, checking people through the thought-link carefully and without pressure or abrupt impulses, trying to sense the characteristic response.

After two days of searching in Prague, he has detected no response. Through the thought-link, he receives short reports from other kharakternyky sent by Dmytro to Warsaw, Berlin, Paris and Vienna. At that point, their results are similarly discouraging: no latent kharakternyky have been found.

ROLE IN THE STORY

A new member of the team

Yaroslav moves from his familiar role as Dmytro's deputy to a new task: finding people whose kharakternyk gift remains latent. His story follows the first steps of a newly initiated kharakternyk, from the shock of initiation to the practical use of the thought-link.

His exact age, family and the full range of his later abilities are not established in the reviewed passages, so they are not added here.
