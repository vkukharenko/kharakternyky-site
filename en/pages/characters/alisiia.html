KHARAKTERNYKY · INITIATION · PORTALS · TRAINING

Alisia

A curious, impulsive young kharakternyk who quickly explores new abilities, is unafraid to act and eventually helps others begin their own journey.

KEY INFORMATION

From first contact to mentorship

Alisia is a Ukrainian teenage girl who becomes a kharakternyk after first sensing a thought sent by her best friend through the thought-link. She is fifteen at the beginning of her storyline. The initiation opens a new world to her, and she is so eager to enter it that she argues with her parents and looks for help on her own.

Alisia gradually learns to open portals, trains with experienced kharakternyky, suffers a severe injury and goes through psychological recovery. Later, she is no longer only a student: she helps other kharakternyky develop their abilities.

Name: Alisia

Origin: Ukraine

Status: initiated kharakternyk

Abilities: thought-link, opening portals and spatial transitions

PERSONALITY AND COMMUNICATION

Energy, curiosity and directness

Alisia is emotional, restless and talkative. She becomes excited by new possibilities, moves quickly from one thought to another and does not always manage to explain the whole train of thought to others. Even through the thought-link, her messages can arrive as a chaotic stream.

She is persistent and reluctant to accept refusal, especially when she believes an opportunity matters. At the same time, she can recognise danger and listen to people she trusts. Her eagerness to act sometimes runs ahead of her experience, so her mentors have to remind her to be careful.

After a severe injury, she goes through a difficult psychological period. Physical recovery does not erase what she remembers, and she needs time before she feels like herself again.

INITIATION AND FIRST STEPS

A new ability changes ordinary life

The first turning point comes when Alisia catches a thought from her best friend. She realises that she has become part of the kharakternyky community. Together, they try to open a portal so they can meet, but their attempts fail, and Alisia decides to find instructors.

At first, her parents do not believe her and refuse to let their daughter go to a space station. Dmytro visits them in person, explains what has happened and helps overcome their distrust. Alisia is then able to begin training and gradually learns to use the abilities of the kharakternyky.

When she first travels through portals on her own, excitement gets ahead of caution. Dmytro catches up with her during a journey through European cities and gives her a long talk about safety and responsibility.

TRAINING WITH THE MOLFAR

Returning to mastery

After psychological trauma, Alisia works with the Molfar to regain her sword skills. Gradually, training becomes more than a way to recover a technique: it helps her feel part of the community again.

Later, she regularly visits the Molfar’s training hall, takes part in sparring and gets to know his students. Her experience becomes useful to others: together with the Molfar, she helps seven students experience the skill of opening portals and take their first steps towards using it independently.

Alisia explains the principles of the thought-link, stresses the need to control one’s own thoughts and respect other people’s mental boundaries. She is no longer just another trainee; she has become a guide for newcomers.

TRIALS AND RECOVERY

Choosing to help despite the danger

During a rescue operation at an acid field, Alisia independently opens portals beneath people trapped in the deadly substance and moves them to safety. She keeps helping even after she herself is caught in danger.

The acid causes severe injuries. Dmytro manages to open a portal and rescue Alisia along with other people. A medical capsule stabilises her, and full recovery takes place at an orbital station.

Although her body recovers, the experience leaves a psychological mark. She instinctively remembers the pain and cannot immediately believe that the danger has passed. Support from people close to her and a gradual return to familiar activities help her through this period.

LATER ROLE

Sharing the experience

In the third book, Alisia continues travelling with the Molfar and a group of his students. She helps the group move between locations, communicates with communities through the thought-link and takes part in training new kharakternyky.

Her journey shows the transition from an impulsive beginner eager to open her first portal to a more experienced kharakternyk who can pass on knowledge and take responsibility for others.

REFERENCE NOTES

Limits of the confirmed canon

Confirmed by the manuscripts
  • Alisia is fifteen at the beginning of her storyline.
  • She has a best friend whose thought she first senses through the thought-link.
  • Her parents initially refuse to let her train, but agree after speaking with Dmytro.
  • She learns to open portals, trains and travels between cities and Plans.
  • The Molfar helps her regain sword skills after psychological trauma.
  • She is severely injured during a rescue operation at an acid field and undergoes medical and psychological recovery.
  • Later, she helps the Molfar train a group of seven students.
Details from the approved visual reference

Light hair tied back, light-coloured eyes, practical dark clothing with Ukrainian elements and equipment. These features come from the approved illustration, not from a complete verbal description in the manuscripts.

Details not established in the text

Her exact height, date of birth, full name, precise place of residence, and the names of her parents and best friend are not established in the reviewed passages. Her age of fifteen is confirmed at the beginning of her storyline; her exact age in later events is not specified.

Appearances across the books

Book 1: initiation, meeting Dmytro, first attempts to open portals, training and independent journeys. Book 2: training with the Molfar, teaching his students, the acid-field rescue operation, medical and psychological recovery. Book 3: travelling with the Molfar and his students, transitions and contact with communities.
