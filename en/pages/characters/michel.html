PROFILE

Pilot on Evihid's team

Michel is a professional pilot and a member of Evihid's team. He knows the ship used by the group and has many years of experience working in emergency situations. As part of the team, he takes part in flights, reconnaissance operations and tests of unfamiliar technology.

TEAM ROLE

An experienced pilot in extraordinary conditions

Michel was selected for an experiment involving the central control seat of a captured alien ship. The scientists considered his professionalism, familiarity with the ship and experience in emergency situations. Although he was understandably sceptical about the risky experiment, he agreed to take part.

During contact with the ship, Michel sensed that the system was trying to understand him just as he was trying to understand it. Although there were no familiar control panels, he began to intuitively recognise the locations of individual systems. This became an important step in testing and learning to operate the unfamiliar technology.

STORY ROLE AND RELATIONSHIPS

A member of the research team

Michel works alongside Evihid, Kamil and the other members of the expedition. In field operations, he supports the team as pilot and ship operator, assesses the craft's capabilities and contributes to decisions about safe movement and evacuation.

His interactions with Kamil in the manuscripts are shown through their shared work aboard the ship: they exchange observations, discuss scan results and assess which discoveries should be transported to Earth for further study.
