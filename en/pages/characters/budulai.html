HORUNZHY OTAMAN · INDUSTRY · INTER-PLAN PORTALS

Budulai

A kharakternyk who combines the coordination of scientific and industrial programmes with practical portal work.

OVERVIEW

Science and industry organiser

Budulai is one of Sukhum's horunzhy otamans. His work spans dozens of parallel tasks: engineers, scientists, laboratory heads and industrial managers are constantly working around him. He oversees calculations and long-term development programmes, assesses the needs of different plans and determines which capacities must be expanded.

Status: horunzhy otaman serving under Sukhum

Main responsibility: scientific, engineering and industrial programmes

Abilities: opening and maintaining inter-plan portals

Age: not specified in the reviewed passages

Appearance: long dark hair tied back, beard and dark clothing with a blue pendant, based on the author's supplied image

WORKING WITH SUKHUM

Responsibility for infrastructure development

After the agreements with the Second Plan, Sukhum gives Budulai a new set of tasks. Budulai assesses what the resettled civilisation will need and explains that it must be provided with residential complexes, medical centres, water purification, transport and energy. At the same time, the army will need armoured suits, weapons, ammunition, vehicles and training equipment.

He proposes expanding production capacity, deploying automated factories and building new energy nodes. He also raises the issue of space security: developing new engines, orbital combat platforms and reserves for future space programmes.

DAY 17 · EVACUATION

Portals under fire

During an attack on a settlement, Budulai organises the evacuation of civilians to a hidden settlement on the planet's other hemisphere. He opens portals one after another, sends groups through and immediately establishes the next passage. On the other side, fighters receive the evacuees and lead them away from the portal.

As the attackers draw closer, Budulai continues working under fire. He helps evacuate children, elderly people and the wounded, coordinating with the fighters defending the site. After each group passes, he closes the portal and opens another for as long as people remain at the evacuation point.

PERSONALITY AND RELATIONSHIPS

Focus and a practical outlook

In conversations, Budulai studies the information he receives carefully and first looks for its practical consequences: what must be built, manufactured or supplied. He thinks in terms of systems and assesses resource constraints in advance. His humour is dry and restrained, but he does not shy away from difficult tasks.

Sukhum is his hetman and direct superior. Nayrin and Iman are the other horunzhy otamans with whom Budulai shares the wider responsibilities. During the evacuation, he works alongside the fighters who secure the perimeter and receive people after the crossing.

The available passages do not specify his age, family or a separate personal backstory. No such details have been added without manuscript support.
