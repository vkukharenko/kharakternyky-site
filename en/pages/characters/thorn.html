MILITARY STORYLINE · SNIPER

Thorn

A hunter who spent years living on the edge of the front line and must now learn to see the world differently.

PROFILE

A sniper with years of combat experience

Thorn is the callsign of an experienced sniper who operated alone in the grey zone for a long time. He knew how to find concealed positions, patiently track targets and survive in places where others would not last even a few days.

Name: Thorn is a callsign; his real name is not revealed

Specialisation: sniping, observation and concealed movement

Previous affiliation: the enemy side

Age: not specified in the manuscript

Health: his body is restored after capture, including an old leg injury

New ability: opening portals

BACKGROUND AND CHARACTER

From conviction to doubt

At the beginning of the war, Thorn joined the fighting voluntarily, believing the claims of “liberation” and the righteousness of his involvement. Over time, the destruction and deaths he witnessed gradually undermined those beliefs. He could not bring himself to question everything he had once believed, and increasingly focused on the war itself.

Thorn is used to thinking coldly, resisting his first impulse and analysing unfamiliar situations. Solitude does not trouble him: fewer people nearby means fewer accidental mistakes. In captivity, he does not panic; instead, he tries to assemble the facts and understand what has happened to him.

SKILLS AND ABILITIES

Sniping and portals

His years of experience are built around choosing positions, judging distance, wind and trajectory, camouflage and disappearing from sight. He received ammunition and supplies from small drones, with almost no direct contact with other people.

After being captured, Thorn discovers that he can open portals. At first, the transitions are slow: he spends too long choosing a destination and tries to make each portal perfect. During training, he learns to treat a portal as a quick change of position. His sniper's way of thinking works naturally with this new ability: he immediately looks for places from which he can observe, remain unseen and control a sector.

EVENTS AND RELATIONSHIPS

Captivity and a new stage

  • During a prolonged confrontation, Thorn hunts a fighter who repeatedly escapes his traps. Both are wounded, and their final encounter ends with Thorn's defeat.
  • After his capture, his body is restored so completely that even the effects of old injuries disappear.
  • Ihor visits him after he wakes. Thorn recognises familiar movements and gradually connects them with his previous experience on the front.
  • During training, Thorn learns to use portals and combine them with his sniper skills.
Details not specified by the manuscript

Thorn's real name, exact age, height, birthplace, family and full military biography are not established. These details have not been added to the canon.
