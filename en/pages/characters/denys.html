Denys

Artem and Vlad's friend. Curious, sociable and often the first to test an interesting idea. Behind his ordinary teenage manner lies extraordinary mental power.

OVERVIEW

Artem and Vlad's friend

Denys appears alongside Artem and Vlad before their move to the underground city. Together they discuss portals, get used to their bracelets and explore their new environment. Denys quickly befriends Kair and often initiates their shared adventures.

Name: Denys

PERSONALITY

Curiosity, initiative and humour

In everyday life, Denys is lively and sociable, easily drawn into new ideas. He suggests exploring unfamiliar sectors, testing unusual possibilities or going somewhere without a strict plan. He enjoys arguing, joking and persuading others, but does not hold on to minor disagreements.

He quickly finds common ground with Kair and tries to introduce him to familiar things. After his experiences in the laboratory, Denys becomes wary of people who hold power over him. He watches their intentions carefully and wants to remain in control of his own decisions.

STORYLINE

From ordinary life to the laboratory

In the earlier parts of the story, Denys is one of Artem and Vlad's friends. Together they explore the underground city, learn about its transport and sectors, and later welcome Kair into their circle.

The third book reveals another side of his story. Denys was held in a laboratory and subjected to cruel experiments intended to intensify and study his mental abilities. To survive, he learned to conceal his true strength and pretend that he could barely use it.

During the final experiment, Denys seized an opportunity, incapacitated the staff and brought the people in the facility under his mental control. After the operation, Olha and Serhii find him. He gradually regains consciousness, undergoes initial checks at the lunar base and begins learning about other worlds.

Olha and Serhii make it clear that they do not intend to use his abilities as a weapon. Denys is free to decide whether he wants to join United Humanity. This is a sharp contrast with the laboratory, where his unusual abilities were treated only as a reason for more experiments.

ABILITIES

Mental influence

Denys can perceive other people's thoughts, emotions and intentions. He can influence behaviour, stop a person's movements, put people to sleep and bring them under his will. The laboratory records and the events during his escape indicate that he can affect a large number of people at once.

The mental protection used by kharakternyky can block his influence. During a test, Olha and Serhii prevented him from establishing a mental contact, and Denys acknowledged that their protection worked.

After years of coercion, Denys is especially sensitive to attempts to control him. His abilities belong to him, and the decision to use them must remain his own.
