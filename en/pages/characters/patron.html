MEXICO · SIERRA HOUSE · NAHUALI

Patron

The leader of the Sierra House, known as El Patrón. A strategist determined to preserve his House's independence and protect its people.

PROFILE

Leader of the Sierra House

The Patron leads the Sierra House in Mexico. He is accustomed to making his own decisions and has no intention of handing control of his House to anyone else. At the same time, he understands that open confrontation with the Order could put the people under his protection at risk.

BACKGROUND

The House under pressure from the Order

The nahuali made the Sierra House a centralised and influential organisation: they are stronger than ordinary people, live longer and maintain connections with other Plans. The Order seeks to control the nahuali and use them to influence the Houses themselves.

The Patron has no intention of handing control of his House to anyone. Open confrontation is dangerous for now, so his priority is to complete the relocation of his people without revealing its true scale.

Ines, a representative of the Order, demands that one or two portal users be placed at the Order's permanent disposal. The Patron does not agree immediately: he understands that such a concession could be the first step towards losing control of his own House.

DECISIONS AND ALLIANCES

Preparing to relocate the House

The Patron consults Esteban, whose experience and abilities he respects. After his initiation in Ukraine, Esteban became the informal leader of the House's newly initiated kharakternyky, while remaining a subordinate of the Patron and a member of the Sierra House.

He asks Esteban to prepare fighters willing to travel to Ukraine for possible initiation and to approach the Ukrainian hetman about sending already initiated kharakternyky. Neither option guarantees a quick result, but the Patron decides to pursue both.

He wants to preserve the House as an independent community: his people can remain part of the Sierra House while also meeting their obligations to the wider kharakternyk community.

PERSONALITY AND VISUAL REFERENCE

Strategy and self-control

In the story, the Patron is a pragmatic and strategic leader. He is restrained in conversation, carefully assesses the balance of power, does not confuse paid security with loyalty and refuses to let pressure push him into careless decisions. At the same time, he values the people of his House and takes responsibility for their safety.
