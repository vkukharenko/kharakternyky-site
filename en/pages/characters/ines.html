THE ORDER · HOUSE SIERRA · NEGOTIATIONS

Ines

An Order representative who visits the Patron of House Sierra to discuss cooperation and secure portalists for the Order.

KEY INFORMATION

Representative of the Order

Ines represents the Order in negotiations with the Patron of House Sierra. Her visit is not limited to discussing formal agreements: she presses the House to demonstrate its willingness to cooperate in practical terms.

During the negotiations, Ines demands that one or two portalists be placed at the Order's disposal for an unspecified period. The demand both tests House Sierra's position and carries an implicit threat.

Name: Ines

ROLE IN THE STORY

Negotiation as a test of willingness to cooperate

Ines arrives at the Patron of House Sierra as a representative of the Order. She discusses agreements while putting pressure on her counterpart to secure a concrete resource: portalists who would be placed at the Order's disposal.

The demand for one or two portalists for an unspecified period has a double meaning: it is an attempt to secure the Order's influence and a test of how far the House is prepared to accept its terms. The diplomatic form of the conversation conceals an implicit threat.

CHARACTER AND BEHAVIOUR

Restrained and demanding

The agreed character description presents Ines as restrained, demanding, focused, confident and diplomatic. She does not avoid applying pressure, but frames her demands as part of a negotiation.
