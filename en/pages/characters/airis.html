Iris

A kharakternyk of the Zero Plan who specialises in complex portal control and the transfer of large structures between plans.

PROFILE

An expert in inter-planar transfers

Iris is a kharakternyk of the Zero Plan. His specialisation is complex portal operations where simply opening a passage at the required point is not enough. He can change a portal’s shape and position while it is moving, align it precisely with a structure and maintain a stable transition throughout a prolonged operation.

Status: Kharakternyk of the Zero Plan

Specialisation: Portal control and inter-planar transfers

Appearance: Tousled dark hair, light stubble and dark clothing, following the approved image

Age: Not specified in the reviewed manuscripts

DAY 7 · TRANSFERRING THE CITY-PATH

A task that could not be entrusted to just anyone

When Sukhum proposed moving a City-Path to the resort planet and arranging it in a ring around the settlements, Iris explained why the standard method used for underground cities would not work. The earlier technology preserved a structure’s original geometry, whereas the City-Path had to change from a linear form into a closed system.

Iris considered using a moving portal. The kharakternyk would remain aboard a shuttle, guide the transition along the structure and continuously reshape it to follow the trajectory of the future ring. The massive supports were especially difficult: the portal would have to be reconfigured around each one without losing stability.

He warned Sukhum that the chance of success was low because the entire burden would fall on one kharakternyk. Even so, Iris volunteered to carry out the operation himself. He did not promise success, but was ready to take personal responsibility for the risk.

DAY 10 · THE TRANSFER BEGINS

Precision at every stage

Preparation revealed a small error in the calculations. Modelling the entire route showed that a minor deviation at the beginning would accumulate and could create a critical load on the City-Path’s trusses by the end. Iris worked with the engineers until the control algorithms were corrected and the calculations were confirmed again.

He departed with pilots and engineers, but without another kharakternyk: Iris had to hold the portal himself, and an additional kharakternyk would not remove the central risk. The engineers monitored the structure and its supports, the pilots kept the shuttle on its calculated course, and technical teams stood ready to intervene if necessary.

As the transfer began, Iris formed a portal around a defined section of the structure. He then held it precisely in relation to the City-Path as the shuttle moved forward. The transition had to travel along the structure without compromising its integrity or the correct distribution of loads between the old and new supports.

TECHNOLOGY AND REBUILDING

Knowledge he is willing to share

When the travellers return, Iris welcomes them alongside Sukhum and Iman and helps them understand a world that has changed over twenty years. He tells them about the City-Paths, their role in human survival and the gradual restoration of connections between surviving sections.

Iris also speaks with evident pride about an experimental technology that can connect an ordinary person to the kharakternyky thought-link through a special bracelet. At that point the system still has limitations: the device is bulky, its battery does not last long, and its use has side effects. Nevertheless, the first person can already use the thought-link almost as fully as a kharakternyk.

CHARACTER

Confidence without overconfidence

Iris pays close attention to technical details and does not hide risks, even when he is willing to take on the most difficult part of a task himself. He checks calculations, refuses unsafe shortcuts and does not avoid personal responsibility. In conversation, Iris is open to new acquaintances, readily explains what has changed and knows how to ease tension with a well-timed joke.
