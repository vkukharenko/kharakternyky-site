MAIN STORY · UNDERGROUND CITIES · MEDICINE

Artem

A young student who gradually discovers an unusual aptitude for medicine. His intuition helps him find the right settings and decisions when working with medical recovery capsules.

OVERVIEW

A student with a medical aptitude

At the beginning of the story, Artem lives an ordinary teenage life. He and his mother go through the procedures at a distribution centre, receive bracelets and later move to a protected underground residential complex. There he continues his education, spends time with his friends and gradually discovers the possibilities of this new environment.

Name: Artem

Age: not specified precisely in the supplied story passages

Family: his mother is with him in the early scenes

Friends: Denys and Vlad; later, Kair

Education: school and subsequent medical training

Main aptitude: intuitive understanding of a patient's condition and medical capsule settings

Training: diagnostics, practical medical equipment work and assisting the wounded

Origin: the supplied passages do not identify his exact place of residence before relocation

PERSONALITY

Attentive, persistent and focused

Artem cannot always explain immediately why he chooses a particular solution. During medical tests, he notices abnormalities in a patient's condition before he can provide a logical explanation. His answers often begin with an intuition about what needs to be checked.

He can become so absorbed in a subject that he puts aside ordinary entertainment. After seeing medical recovery capsules, Artem returns to the medical complex on his own, even though his friends suggest spending time elsewhere. At the same time, he remains part of the group and enjoys their shared explorations.

STORYLINE

From relocation to medical training

In the first book, Artem is shown during a period when war is gradually changing everyday life. He visits a distribution centre with his mother, receives a bracelet and waits to be relocated to a protected residential complex. His friends Denys and Vlad are close to him during this period.

In the underground city, Artem continues his education. With his friends, he explores new sectors, becomes familiar with the medical infrastructure and sees recovery capsules in operation for the first time. They capture his attention and gradually become the focus of his interest.

During later assessments, medical staff notice that Artem can intuitively identify problems in diagnostic images and find the right capsule settings. He is invited to additional tests and later begins training at a medical institute, including practical classes.

In the third book, Artem and Kair are already working with medical recovery capsules in practice. When the resort planet urgently needs medical personnel, the boys go with the medical team. During the fighting, they help treat the wounded and independently select capsule modes, checking each other's work.

ABILITIES AND SKILLS

Medical intuition

During assessments, Artem can identify areas of injury and notice signs that point to a problem. He cannot always explain his reasoning, but often reaches the correct conclusion before a conventional analysis is complete.

His practical aptitude becomes clear when working with medical recovery capsules: he understands patient parameters, selects suitable modes and monitors the recovery process. In a crisis, he remains composed and can work without constant guidance from an instructor.

The supplied passages establish medical intuition and a rapid ability to master the equipment. They do not assign Artem a separate supernatural power or other abilities.
