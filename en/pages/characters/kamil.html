Kamil

A technical specialist on Evihid's team, helping investigate unfamiliar worlds and alien technologies.

PROFILE

Neuroengineer and investigator of alien systems

Kamil, callsign Sigma, is a neuroengineer and a member of Evihid's regular reconnaissance team, alongside the pilot Michelle. Her work involves analysing scan data, neural interfaces, thought-link systems and complex technical systems.

BACKGROUND AND CHARACTER

Attention to detail and a research mindset

Kamil works in a team whose members know one another well and have extensive experience operating together. During reconnaissance, she analyses scanner data, is among the first to notice signs of biological activity and develops technical conclusions from observed evidence.

The manuscripts reveal her character primarily through her professional conduct: attentiveness, technical competence, caution in drawing conclusions and a willingness to test hypotheses.

SKILLS AND SPECIALISATION

Neural interfaces and bio-organic technology

During the survey of a planet recently captured by the aliens, Kamil works with scan data and detects biological activity in an unusual terraforming system. Later, after the orbital battle, she joins the investigation of a captured alien vessel.

Kamil analyses its organic conductors and energy system, works with engineers on an intermediate interface between human electronics and the alien biosystem, and manually adjusts its operating parameters.

EVENTS AND RELATIONSHIPS

Evihid's team

  • A regular member of Evihid's reconnaissance team, working alongside him and the pilot Michelle.
  • Takes part in a mission to a planet recently captured by the aliens, helping investigate traces of their activity.
  • After the orbital battle above Earth, joins the survey of the wreckage and the investigation of an almost intact alien vessel.
  • Helps decipher its power systems and develop an interface for interacting with the vessel's biosystems.
