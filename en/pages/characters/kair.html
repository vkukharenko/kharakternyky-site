ZERO PLAN · UNDERGROUND CITY

Kair

An observant and practical teenager who grew up in an underground city. He helps his friends see a familiar environment in a new way and later studies medical capsules alongside Artem.

OVERVIEW

A boy from the Zero Plan

Kair comes from the Zero Plan and has spent his entire life in an underground city. Its infrastructure, transport and technical systems are ordinary parts of everyday life to him. When he meets Artem, Denys and Vlad, his experience helps them understand the city better.

Name: Kair

Origin: Zero Plan

Age: not specified precisely in the text

Appearance: thick dark curly hair, dark eyes and tan skin — based on the supplied visual reference

Visual outfit: dark T-shirt, charcoal hoodie and practical casual clothing

Close circle: Artem, Denys and Vlad

Studies: training that includes medical preparation and capsule operation

Notable traits: attention to detail, strong observation and quick recognition of relevant indicators

PERSONALITY

A calm observer with a practical outlook

Kair does not rush to speak, but carefully watches people and his surroundings. He notices changes in how systems work, listens to technical sounds and quickly finds his way around a familiar environment. In unfamiliar situations he remains composed and usually answers briefly.

As he spends time with Artem, Denys and Vlad, he becomes more open. His reserve is balanced by a sense of humour and a willingness to join friendly arguments. He does not impose his habits on his friends, but calmly explains why things work the way they do.

STORYLINE

Discovering a new world

In the second book, Kair meets Artem, Denys and Vlad and shows them the underground city not merely as a collection of corridors and sectors, but as a living place with its own systems, workshops and everyday routes. The boys explore the city together, travel by train and eventually go to the surface. This is also a special experience for Kair: he is used to underground life and sees open space, the sky and ordinary surface-world things differently.

During training, Kair demonstrates a keen eye for medical indicators. In practical exercises, he notices important signs that other students miss and reaches correct conclusions before all the data has been fully analysed. His instructor specifically notes his ability to identify relevant indicators among many others.

In the third book, during the attack on the resort planet, Kair and Artem are attending medical training in the underground city on the First Plan. They assist the medical staff by checking capsule parameters and selecting suitable modes for the wounded. Even as explosions strike nearby, the boys continue to work carefully and without panic.

SKILLS

Observation and medical capsule operation

The text presents Kair as a perceptive observer who understands technical environments and notices details others overlook. He can identify an unusual sound from an ageing system and assess its condition from indirect signs.

During medical training, Kair demonstrates an intuitive understanding of patient indicators and medical capsule settings. Together with Artem, he selects capsule modes for wounded people. The supplied passages describe this as quick analysis and practical application of knowledge; they do not establish a separate supernatural power for Kair.

VISUAL REFERENCE

Appearance and style

Appearance

The visual reference establishes thick dark curly hair, dark eyes, tan skin and youthful facial features. Numerical age, height and exact physical measurements are not confirmed by the story text.

Clothing and colour palette

The main look is a dark T-shirt and charcoal hoodie. The palette is restrained: black, graphite, dark grey and natural skin tones.

Canon and sources

The storyline has been checked against the supplied editions of books two and three. Book two contains Kair's introduction to the friends, underground-city life, shared outings and medical training. Book three shows him working with medical equipment during an attack. Appearance and clothing are based on the author's visual reference; unsupported numerical details have not been invented.
