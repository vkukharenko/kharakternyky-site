EMPIRE · SARGI · MERCENARIES

Keor

A Sarg mercenary of the Empire. A professional fighter who places the completion of a mission above political disputes.

PROFILE

A mercenary of the Sarg race

Keor belongs to the Sargi, a sentient humanoid race found across many Plans of the Empire. He works as a mercenary and undertakes assignments under contract.

Race: Sarg

Status: Mercenary

Affiliation: The Empire

Role: Contract military missions

CHARACTER AND PRINCIPLES

A professional first

Keor is not concerned with which side is right or what political cause lies behind a conflict. He is a mercenary: he receives an assignment, carries it out and judges the result. He feels no personal hostility towards those on the other side once the contract has ended.

He notices competence and respects the ability to act. After witnessing the humans' fighting tactics, Keor becomes genuinely interested in their way of warfare and even offers Dmytro a place on a mercenary team.

MEETING DMYTRO

The mission is over

After the mission ends unsuccessfully, Keor finds himself far from the military base, severely wounded. Dmytro could have simply walked away, but chooses to help him. That changes the nature of their encounter: Keor no longer regards Dmytro as an enemy.

During their conversation, Keor explains the Empire, its structure, the prince who rules several Plans, and the rules governing transitions. He is surprised that Dmytro has no translator and initially assumes he comes from a world far removed from Imperial culture.

APPEARANCE

Keor is a Sarg with the race's distinctly non-human physiology: dense skin formed into overlapping armour-like plates, an elongated head with bony ridges and red eyes. His build is massive and athletic. Battle armour makes his silhouette heavier without making him look like a human wearing a suit.
