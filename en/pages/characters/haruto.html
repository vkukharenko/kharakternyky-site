KHARAKTERNYKY · JAPAN

Haruto

A student of Molfar from Japan. Reserved and observant, he notices details that others often miss.

PROFILE

A Japanese student of Molfar

Haruto is one of Molfar's seven strongest students. He is reserved and rarely joins conversations, but carefully watches what is happening around him. In the story, he is described as someone who notices almost every detail.

Country: Japan

Age: 17

Height: 176 cm

Build: slim, enduring

Eyes: dark brown

Hair: black, straight, medium length, slightly tousled

Dominant hand: right

Status: initiated

Ability: thought-link (telepathic connection)

PERSONALITY

Observes more than most

Haruto is reserved and attentive to detail. He rarely speaks, but when he joins a conversation, his words are usually precise. He is analytical and logical, and can remain calm in difficult situations.

He does not seek attention among the other students. Instead, he listens, analyses and notices things that may escape everyone else's attention.

TRAINING AND ABILITIES

Thought-link

After his initiation, Haruto discovers the thought-link together with Molfar's other students. During an exercise, he sends Eero the image of a snow-covered forest, demonstrating that he can communicate through thoughts rather than spoken words.

Haruto later notes that thoughts can overlap when several people share the connection. He understands the importance of control: one must learn not only to transmit information, but also to limit what one receives from others.

Haruto belongs to Molfar's group of seven strongest students, who have enough experience to act independently in dangerous situations.
