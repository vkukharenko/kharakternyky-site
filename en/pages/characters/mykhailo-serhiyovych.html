Mykhailo Serhiyovych

A kharakternyk and senior government official working to bring the state system into the new society and oppose the Order from within.

KEY INFORMATION

A government official and kharakternyk

Mykhailo Serhiyovych is a senior government official who joins the kharakternyky community after initiation. He knows the old system of power, its internal arrangements and weak points.

His area is government and public administration. Dmytro oversees key operatives, including Mykhailo, while Mykhailo coordinates the government effort.

Name: Mykhailo Serhiyovych

Status: Kharakternyk, government official

Area: Public administration

Key role: First social agreement

Age and height: Not established

ROLE IN THE STORY

The first social agreement

After initiation, Mykhailo initially switches off the thought-link. He fears that complete openness could expose corrupt arrangements and destabilise the state faster than the war.

On behalf of the community, Olha agrees to grant full amnesty for past actions to Mykhailo and those who voluntarily join through him, on the condition of full openness and cooperation for the shared society. This becomes the first social agreement.

He later works with civil servants and officials, including people with a history of corruption. Amnesty does not excuse their past actions; it is granted in exchange for full cooperation and honest work in the new system.

GOVERNMENT WORK

Rebuilding the government from within

Mykhailo works almost without rest, explains the changes to thousands of people and brings public administrators into the community. He knows the old system and its weak points.

When Order agents are found embedded in ministries, administrations and services, he coordinates the identification and detention of key members. Known agents are isolated while operations are disguised as routine state inspections.

CHARACTER AND APPEARANCE

A restrained administrator

In his first conversation with Olha, he is described as a sturdy, slightly stocky man with an attentive gaze and a precise manner of speaking. He is used to controlling situations and making decisions, although after initiation he experiences inner tension.

Visual design

The approved reference depicts him with short dark hair, wearing a dark business suit, white shirt and tie, with a small gold trident on the lapel. These are visual-reference details, not additional facts of book canon.

Abilities and personal belongings

The story confirms that he has kharakternyk abilities and a thought-link after initiation, but initially keeps it switched off and must learn to control his new abilities. The available excerpts provide no separate list of special abilities or personal belongings.

Canon and limits of confirmed information

The story confirms his status as a government official and kharakternyk, the first social agreement, government coordination, work with amnestied officials and opposition to the Order's agents. His exact age, height and personal belongings are not detailed in the available excerpts.
