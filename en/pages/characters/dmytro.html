CHARACTER STORY

Who is Dmytro?

Dmytro's story begins with an initiation that changes his life and reveals the world of the kharakternyky and the existence of other Plans.

On the Zero Plan, he trains, adapts to new abilities and gradually becomes involved in events far beyond his personal story.

Role in the events

Dmytro takes part in transitions between Plans, evacuation and military decisions, and the development of cooperation between the Central Plans.
