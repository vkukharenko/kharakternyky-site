Dmytro

A former volunteer and IT specialist whose ordinary life changes after a mysterious passage into another world.

PROFILE

An IT specialist with military experience

Dmytro is thirty-six at the beginning of the story. He has sharp facial features, an attentive, slightly tired gaze and short stubble with the first traces of grey. In everyday life, he looks unremarkable: neat, simple clothes and a shoulder bag.

He volunteered for military service in 2014 and fought in the Luhansk region. After returning, he rarely speaks about what he experienced and goes back to working at a computer. His professional world is servers, protocols and secure communication channels.

THE BEGINNING

Between ordinary life and a new world

Before the events of the story, Dmytro works on a classified military project. One morning, while walking back from a café, he is caught in an enemy drone strike. He regains consciousness in a dark cave and gradually realises that he is not beneath rubble but somewhere entirely different.

Rather than panic, Dmytro tries to assess the situation methodically and find a way out. His habit of analysing circumstances and looking for practical solutions becomes important in an unfamiliar environment.

INITIATION AND TRAINING

New physical capabilities

After initiation, Dmytro notices that he moves faster and reacts more precisely. Airis explains that his body has been rebuilt, but warns him that speed alone does not guarantee victory. To understand his new limits, Dmytro undergoes practical training: first testing coordination and reaction through table tennis, then working on endurance and running.

Later, he uses combat training, a sabre and a combat exoskeleton. In fights, Dmytro does not rely on strength alone: he changes his angle of attack, looks for an opponent's weak points and adapts to their tactics.

CHARACTER AND BEHAVIOUR

Observant and self-controlled

Dmytro pays attention to details and is used to acting without unnecessary fuss. Even during an air-raid alert, he estimates the distance to shelter and moves quickly without panicking. At the same time, he does not see himself as a hero and is not inclined to exaggerate his abilities.

His military background and technical mindset complement each other: he can work with information, analyse a situation and remain focused when circumstances become dangerous.
