Ragar

A Sarg commander of a mercenary squad. An experienced leader, strategist and negotiator.

PROFILE

Commander of a mercenary squad

Ragar is a Sarg who commands his own mercenary squad. His unit has transport and equipment, takes contracts from different rulers and does not bind itself to a single patron.

Species: Sarg

Status: Mercenary squad commander

Role: Leader, strategist, negotiator

Connections: Acquainted with Keor

CHARACTER AND PRINCIPLES

An experienced commander

Ragar is experienced, measured and pragmatic. He takes responsibility for his people, assesses risks and tries to negotiate terms before work begins. If a client changes the agreement during an assignment, Ragar is prepared to renegotiate the payment.

He does not hide the fact that not every contract can simply be refused. His attitude differs from Keor’s, who points out that a mercenary can sometimes decline an assignment.

MEETING DMYTRO

An offer to join the squad

Ragar meets Keor in a city bar and learns that he is travelling with Dmytro, the human who helped him after he was wounded. Intrigued by this unusual companion, Ragar asks about his combat skills and ability to open transitions.

Seeing a potentially valuable fighter in Dmytro, Ragar offers him a place in his squad. He invites Dmytro to meet the team the following day and consider the terms. Dmytro does not give an immediate answer.

APPEARANCE

In the supplied visual dossier, Ragar is depicted as a massive Sarg with grey scaled skin, red eyes, a thick grey beard and long segmented tendrils swept back. He wears layered dark armour and a deep red cloak. A thought-link bracelet is visible on his wrist.
