CANONICAL APPEARANCE

LORD OLAF

Memory of the old war

Olaf is a grey-haired man with a neatly trimmed beard. During the Council, the Supreme addresses him personally and recalls the fate of his son, who died in the old war with the Zero-worlders.

Status: Council lord.

Appearance: Grey hair and a neat beard.

Personal loss: His son died in the old war.

COUNCIL OF LORDS

Personal memory

When the Supreme recalls the old war, Olaf answers without hesitation that he remembers his son's fate well. To him, the Zero-worlders are not an abstract enemy from distant history but part of his personal memory.
