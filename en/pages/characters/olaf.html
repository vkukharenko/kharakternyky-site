LORDS' COUNCIL · MILITARY COMMAND

Lord Olaf

Commander of the Supreme's air forces.

PROFILE

Commander of the air forces

Lord Olaf is responsible for the Supreme's aviation. He directs the deployment of combat aircraft through portals, assigns groups to different routes and coordinates strikes against ground forces and defensive positions. In combat, he remains composed and does not rush to commit all his forces at once.

Title: Lord

Position: Commander of the air forces

Reports to: The Supreme

Coordinates with: Lord Tayles, Lord Alister and Lord Dark

DAY 13 · THE LORDS' COUNCIL

A loss he remembers

During the council, the Supreme reminds Olaf of the fate of his son. Olaf answers briefly and firmly: he remembers very well. The exchange makes clear that the conflict with the Zero Ones carries a personal cost for him.

DAY 17 · AVIATION

Striking from the air

After receiving the Supreme's order, Olaf deploys the air force through portals. He does not let the pilots rush into battle as one mass: the groups take their assigned routes, gain altitude and move into position. Attacks from several directions force the air defences to divide their attention between multiple threats.

Later, the Supreme orders him to concentrate the aircraft on the city-path and exhaust its defences instead of dispersing the force against secondary targets.

DAY 19 · THE COST OF DEFEAT

The aftermath

At the meeting after the defeat, Olaf reports that no more than five per cent of the air fleet survived, and that too few experienced pilots remain to fly even those aircraft. He speaks evenly, but everyone present knows that this calm tone means he is furious. Even Lord Dark is wary of him at such moments.
