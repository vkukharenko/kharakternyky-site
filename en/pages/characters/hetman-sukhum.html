Hetman Sukhum

The hetman of the Zero Plan. A composed and decisive leader who combines strategic vision with practical decisions on defence, cooperation and evacuation.

KEY INFORMATION

Leader of the Zero Plan

Sukhum is the hetman of the Zero Plan civilisation. He leads its community of kharakternyky and helps determine its strategic direction. His work spans inter-Plan connections, reconnaissance, defence, production and organising assistance for other worlds.

Name: Sukhum

Title: Hetman

World: Zero Plan

Status: Kharakternyk of the highest rank

Age: Not stated in the manuscripts

Appearance: Blue skin, long grey hair, a thick white beard and light-coloured eyes

Voice: Low, slightly hoarse

CHARACTER AND LEADERSHIP

Composure, experience and responsibility

Sukhum speaks slowly and confidently, without trying to impress others by displaying his authority. He carefully assesses people and situations, focuses on the substance of a problem and moves quickly from agreements to organising practical work.

On strategic matters, he thinks on the scale of entire civilisations. At the same time, he does not interfere with his subordinates’ decisions without cause: he assesses the wider picture, monitors operations and leaves responsibility with those directly leading them.

CAPABILITIES AND WORK

Inter-Plan coordination

Sukhum uses portals and the thought-link. He works with holographic projections, maps of worlds and reconnaissance data. During his meeting with Hetman Mykolai, he adds new regions to the map using information gathered by the Zero Plan’s scouts.

As a leader, Sukhum coordinates the chorunzhyi and those responsible for individual areas of work. After reaching an agreement with the Second Plan, he initiates preparations for joint operations, commits industrial capacity and organises civilian evacuation programmes to the resort planet.

ROLE IN THE STORY

The alliance of three worlds

Sukhum confirms that the connection between his world and Hetman Mykolai’s world appeared only recently. He also tells them about a third civilisation under attack by the horned warriors. Recognition of this shared threat becomes the basis for an alliance between the three worlds.

In later events, Sukhum takes part in negotiations with Raul, represents the interests of the Zero Plan and supports the creation of a shared coordination mechanism. He stresses that each world must retain control over its internal affairs, while shared military, resource and inter-Plan matters require coordinated decisions.

Key events
  • At his first meeting with Hetman Mykolai, he presents the map of worlds explored by the Zero Plan and explains the emergence of a new connection between the civilisations.
  • Together with Mykolai, he recognises the shared threat to the three central worlds and lays the foundations of their alliance.
  • After negotiations with Raul, he organises the contribution of Zero Plan resources to the joint defence effort.
  • He assigns Nairin, Iman and other area leaders to prepare the work required by the agreements.
  • He takes part in shaping a shared system for managing inter-Plan operations without proposing to abolish the independence of the governments.
  • During combat events, he analyses the situation, receives reports and coordinates follow-up actions and defensive preparations.
Relationships
  • Hetman Mykolai: mutual respect develops quickly between them; they become allies and coordinate the actions of the three worlds.
  • Nairin, Iman and Budulai: chorunzhyi otamans with whom Sukhum coordinates the execution of strategic tasks.
  • Raul: leader of the Second Plan and negotiating partner on joint defence and coordination.
  • Dmytro: Sukhum receives his reports on combat operations and assesses his decisions as an operational leader.
Visual canon

Blue skin, long grey hair brushed back, a thick white beard and light, almost icy eyes. In the author-approved visual sheet, Sukhum wears dark decorated armour with a blue emblem on the chest.
