SECOND PLAN · HUMANITY'S LEADERSHIP

Raul

Chief coordinator of the Second Plan. A political leader balancing the civilisation's security, the interests of its people and joint decisions across three worlds.

OVERVIEW

Chief coordinator of the Second Plan

Raul leads the coordination of humanity on the Second Plan. He represents its interests in negotiations with leaders of other worlds, helps shape a shared strategy and aligns political and practical decisions during wartime.

Name: Raul

Position: Chief coordinator of the Second Plan

Responsibilities: political coordination, defence, evacuation and inter-Plan cooperation

Key allies: Ramos, Sukhum, Mykolai and leaders of other areas

Appearance: mature man with dark, greying hair and a short beard

Height: approximately 183 cm according to the visual reference

Clothing: dark formal uniform with a high collar and metal insignia

PERSONALITY AND LEADERSHIP

A cool head and a strong sense of responsibility

In negotiations and council meetings, Raul comes across as measured and pragmatic. He does not accept major proposals simply because they sound appealing: he first examines weaknesses, resources, risks and consequences for the population. At the same time, once he sees a realistic chance, he is prepared to support even an exceptionally difficult joint plan.

Raul understands the political pressure within his own world and how difficult it is to explain the threat and the need for unpopular decisions to the public. He chooses to tell the truth gradually, taking society's readiness into account.

STORYLINE

From preparation to joint defence

Even before the Horned fleet's open attack, Raul takes part in preparing the Second Plan for war. He coordinates cooperation with the kharakternyky and accepts assistance needed to strengthen the defence. In conversations with Sukhum, he acknowledges that some politicians may see evacuation as their only chance to survive, and that persuading them will not be easy.

During negotiations on uniting the efforts of the three Plans, Raul carefully examines the proposed strategy, asks detailed questions and suggests changes together with Ramos. He eventually agrees to commit the Second Plan's army and direct its industrial capacity toward the shared defence effort.

As events continue, Raul coordinates practical consequences of the war: receiving prisoners, establishing temporary camps, providing essentials and separating certain groups according to risk. He remains one of the leaders receiving reports and expected to make decisions.

STRATEGIC ROLE

Representative of the Second Plan

Raul works at the intersection of politics, military coordination and civilian administration. He accounts for the Second Plan's powerful army and defence infrastructure, as well as its limited space capability. This makes cooperation with the technologically advanced Zero Plan and the kharakternyky of the First Plan strategically important.

After the large-scale evacuation begins, Raul assigns specialists from his Plan to assess how civilians can be protected on the resort planet. He does not propose ready-made schemes without sufficient data; he first seeks to understand the enemy and the conditions of the future defence.

VISUAL REFERENCE

Appearance and design

Appearance and uniform

The visual design establishes a mature man with dark hair, greying at the temples, a short salt-and-pepper beard and a stern gaze. He wears a dark formal uniform with a high collar, restrained metal details and the insignia of the Second Plan.

Posture and expressions

Key poses include standing with his hands behind his back, seated at a council table and working with a tactical projection. His expressions are restrained: concentration, assessment, doubt and resolve.

Canon and sources

The supplied books identify Raul as the chief coordinator of the Second Plan; earlier scenes also refer to him as head of the Council. Appearance, uniform and approximate height come from the author's visual reference, not from a prose description.
