THE AUTHOR

About Me

My name is Volodymyr Kukharenko. I am the author of the Kharakternyky literary universe.

Kharakternyky began as a single story, but gradually grew into a much larger world with its own history, civilizations, characters and events separated by centuries.

The novel Kharakternyky: The Beginning tells the central story, while Stories of Worlds explores other eras and parts of this universe.

THE PROJECT

Kharakternyky

This is not the story of a single hero—or even a single world. Events from different eras gradually form one larger picture, and the stories of individuals become part of something much greater.
