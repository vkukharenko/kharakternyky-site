TECHNOLOGY 02 · PROTECTION

Combat Exosuit

A combat system combining armour, servos, stabilisation, sensors and a tactical interface.

The combat exosuit combines protective armour with powered movement, stabilisation, sensors and a tactical interface. It is designed as an integrated system rather than a standalone suit of armour.

Protection

Armour forms the protective layer of the system.

Powered movement

Servos assist movement and support the wearer under load.

Stabilisation

Stabilisation systems help the wearer control movement and maintain a firing position.

Sensors and interface

Sensor data and the tactical interface support situational awareness.
