TECHNOLOGY OF THE ZERO PLAN

Combat Exosuit

An integrated personal combat platform.

The Combat Exosuit is not merely a set of protective armour; it is a complete combat system. It combines an exoskeleton, armour, movement stabilisation, sensors, a tactical interface, thermal masking, communications and integration with other combat systems.

Exoskeleton is the powered mechanical component of the suit. Combat exosuit is the complete combat system.

The defining feature is not any single component, but the integration of all subsystems into one platform.

Exoskeleton

Servos compensate for the mass of the suit and amplify the wearer's movements. This allows heavier armour configurations without a proportional increase in physical effort.

The system can be reconfigured for different suit builds. Increasing compensation for heavier armour also increases energy consumption.

Movement and stabilisation

The suit uses movement stabilisation, step stabilisers and adaptive shock absorption.

It recognises the surface and automatically accounts for ice, snow, crusted snow, roots and other uneven ground while moving.

Protection

Protection is based on composite elements and energy-absorbing inserts. The front and sides can be reinforced with additional armour layers.

Additional armour increases mass, restricts the field of view, loads the neck and raises the energy demand on the servos.

Local energy protection

Selected configurations can use Zero Plan energy modules to create a local protective field around the head.

This is not a full force shield. It cannot guarantee protection against a direct hit, but may disperse part of the impact energy.

Sensors and HUD

The transparent armoured visor displays an integrated tactical picture: terrain maps, vehicles, thermal traces, mines, drones, obstacles and possible routes.

Information from the suit's own sensors and reconnaissance drones is combined into a shared observation system.

Thermal masking

The suit can reduce the wearer's thermal signature. Heat may be dispersed into the ground, cold air, tree trunks and other parts of the environment.

The goal is not absolute invisibility, but reducing thermal visibility to hostile sensors.

Integration

The combat exosuit operates as part of a unified combat network. It integrates with thought-link, reconnaissance drones, observation systems and weapons.

Soldier + exoskeleton + armour + sensors + HUD + drones + thought-link + weapons.

Tactical role

Assault

Combines protection, enhanced movement, stabilisation and tactical information.

Reconnaissance

Uses sensors, a thermal channel and integration with reconnaissance drones.

Difficult terrain

Surface recognition, step stabilisers and adaptive shock absorption assist movement.

Coordinated combat

Thought-link and combined data sources create a shared tactical picture.

Canonical terminology

Combat exosuit — the complete combat system.

Exoskeleton — the powered mechanical component that supports and amplifies movement.

Armour — the physical protective layer.

Helmet / HUD — the head protection and information system.

Sensors — the system for observing and analysing the environment.

Thought-link — the networked communication and interaction system.
