TECHNOLOGY OF THE ZERO PLAN

Needle Launcher

A universal kinetic delivery platform.

The Needle Launcher is a compact, universal weapon of the Zero Plan, built around a high-efficiency electromagnetic accelerator. Its defining feature is not merely the high velocity of its kinetic projectile, but the ability to program the acceleration profile for the payload being launched.

The Needle Launcher is more than a weapon that fires needles. It is a universal launch system that can deliver different payloads to a selected point at a required speed and acceleration profile.

A single platform can use light anti-personnel needles, heavy kinetic penetrators, sensors, repeaters, smoke capsules, electronic-warfare modules, beacons and transport capsules carrying microdrones.

How the Needle Launcher works

A pulse accumulator inside the weapon is charged gradually by the power unit. It then releases energy to the accelerator over an extremely short interval.

The weapon's electronics identify the installed payload and set the required acceleration profile. A light needle, a heavy penetrator and a microdrone therefore use fundamentally different launch modes.

For a universal system, the goal is not simply to reach the highest possible velocity, but to apply acceleration that the payload can withstand.

Ammunition and modules

01

Light needle

The standard anti-personnel projectile. Its very low mass allows the main cassette to hold around a thousand needles.

Designed to engage personnel and exploit weaker areas of protection.

02

Heavy penetrator

A long kinetic rod with greater mass and substantially higher shot energy.

Used against heavy combat suits, light armoured vehicles and vulnerable structural components.

03

Special modules

A universal cassette for auxiliary tasks: sensors, repeaters, beacons, smoke and aerosol capsules, electronic-warfare modules, decoys and microdrone delivery capsules.

Special payloads

Sensor

A disposable observation device delivered to a position to collect information.

Repeater

Extends communications coverage or relays a signal from behind an obstacle.

Smoke or aerosol capsule

Creates local visual obscuration or releases an aerosol for a specific tactical task.

Electronic-warfare module

Produces local interference or supports electronic countermeasures.

Beacon

Marks a location for friendly forces or compatible systems.

Microdrone

A transport capsule releases a small drone close to the target area.

Cassette system

Interchangeable cassettes allow the operator to select a payload for the task without replacing the whole weapon. The system must identify the cassette and configure the launch cycle accordingly.

Power and thermal management

The pulse accumulator stores energy before a shot and releases it rapidly. The phase-change thermal accumulator absorbs short thermal peaks, but repeated intensive firing still creates a heat-management constraint.

Thermal mode

The weapon's rate of fire depends on the energy required by each launch and the system's ability to remove or store the generated heat.

Emergency cooling

Emergency cooling protects the system from overheating; it is a safeguard, not a way to ignore the thermal limits of sustained fire.

Recoil and acoustic signature

The electromagnetic launch system reduces the characteristic recoil of a conventional firearm, but does not make the weapon physically or acoustically undetectable. The launch cycle, moving mechanisms and impact of the projectile can still reveal the firing position.

Tactical applications

Reconnaissance

Delivering sensors or microdrones beyond an obstacle or into a difficult-to-reach area.

Communications

Placing a repeater or beacon where direct access is dangerous.

Low-profile movement

Using a suitable payload to support a task while limiting the need to expose the operator.

Electronic warfare

Deploying a local electronic-warfare module near the required area.

Engaging equipment

Using heavy penetrators against vulnerable parts of equipment and light armour, within the weapon's established limits.

Characterники and the Needle Launcher

Integration with thought-link systems allows the weapon to exchange information with the operator and other connected equipment.

System limitations

The Needle Launcher is versatile, but it is not limitless. Payload mass, required acceleration, energy reserves, thermal capacity, cassette type and the target's protection all constrain its use. A special payload does not automatically inherit the performance of a light needle.

Canon of Kharakternyky

This page describes the Needle Launcher as a universal kinetic delivery platform of the Zero Plan. Where exact physical parameters are not established in the story canon, they are intentionally not presented as fixed specifications.
