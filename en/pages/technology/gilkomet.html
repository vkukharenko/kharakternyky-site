TECHNOLOGY OF THE ZERO PLAN

Needle Launcher

A universal kinetic delivery platform.

The Needle Launcher is a compact, universal weapon of the Zero Plan, built around a high-efficiency electromagnetic accelerator. Its defining feature is not merely the high velocity of its kinetic projectile, but the ability to program the acceleration profile for the payload being launched.

The Needle Launcher is more than a weapon that fires needles. It is a universal launch system that can deliver different payloads to a selected point at a required speed and acceleration profile.

A single platform can use light anti-personnel needles, heavy kinetic penetrators, sensors, repeaters, smoke capsules, electronic-warfare modules, beacons and transport capsules carrying microdrones.

How the Needle Launcher works

A pulse accumulator inside the weapon is charged gradually by the power unit. It then releases energy to the accelerator over an extremely short interval.

The weapon's electronics identify the installed payload and set the required acceleration profile. A light needle, a heavy penetrator and a microdrone therefore use fundamentally different launch modes.

For a universal system, the goal is not simply to reach the highest possible velocity, but to apply acceleration that the payload can withstand.

Designed to engage personnel and exploit weaker areas of protection.

A long kinetic rod with greater mass and substantially higher shot energy.

Used against heavy combat suits, light armoured vehicles and vulnerable structural components.

A universal cassette for auxiliary tasks: sensors, repeaters, beacons, smoke and aerosol capsules, electronic-warfare modules, decoys and microdrone delivery capsules.

Special payloads

Sensor

A disposable observation device delivered to a position to collect information.

Repeater

Extends communications coverage or relays a signal from behind an obstacle.

Smoke or aerosol capsule

Creates local visual obscuration or releases an aerosol for a specific tactical task.

Electronic-warfare module

Produces local interference or supports electronic countermeasures.

Beacon

Marks a location for friendly forces or compatible systems.

Microdrone

A transport capsule releases a small drone close to the target area.

Cassette system

Interchangeable cassettes allow the operator to select a payload for the task without replacing the whole weapon. The system must identify the cassette and configure the launch cycle accordingly.

Power and thermal management

The pulse accumulator stores energy before a shot and releases it rapidly. The phase-change thermal accumulator absorbs short thermal peaks, but repeated intensive firing still creates a heat-management constraint.

Thermal mode

The weapon's rate of fire depends on the energy required by each launch and the system's ability to remove or store the generated heat.

Emergency cooling

Emergency cooling protects the system from overheating; it is a safeguard, not a way to ignore the thermal limits of sustained fire.

Recoil and acoustic signature

The electromagnetic launch system reduces the characteristic recoil of a conventional firearm, but does not make the weapon physically or acoustically undetectable. The launch cycle, moving mechanisms and impact of the projectile can still reveal the firing position.

Light needle: terminal effect

The light needle's effectiveness is not explained by its small contact area alone. It depends on the combined effect of mass, velocity, length, density, material strength and penetrator geometry.

The anti-personnel projectile is designed differently from an armour-piercing penetrator. After passing through protection, it must transfer its energy effectively to the target rather than simply pass through it.

Heavy penetrator: limits

The heavy projectile is not a thin needle but a long kinetic rod designed specifically to work against armour. It carries no explosive charge: its mass acts as a penetrator and transfers kinetic energy on impact.

Limitation: a handheld Needle Launcher is not a universal answer to every armoured vehicle. Its heavy mode is intended for heavy combat suits, light vehicles, joints, moving components and other weaker areas of protection. The frontal armour of a main battle tank requires a weapon of a different scale.

How special payloads work

Sensor: a compact optical, thermal or acoustic unit can be delivered to a roof, beyond a fence, into a corridor or to the other side of a street.

Repeater: a small communications node automatically joins the tactical network and provides an additional connection point behind substantial obstacles.

Smoke or aerosol capsule: delivers a source of smoke or aerosol to a selected location. The launcher provides accurate delivery; it does not change the properties of the aerosol itself.

Electronic-warfare module: a compact jammer can be deployed ahead of a unit or beside enemy equipment to create a local interference zone affecting drones or communications.

Beacon: creates an additional reference point for the tactical network, navigation, target marking or unit coordination.

Microdrone: the drone is launched inside a transport capsule. The launcher applies a softer acceleration profile; the capsule then opens and the drone begins controlled flight under its own power.

Automatic cassette selection

Different payloads have different shapes, so the Needle Launcher uses a modular feed system. The main cassette holds approximately 1,000 light needles; the heavy cassette carries kinetic penetrators; the special cassette carries sensors, drones, electronic-warfare modules, beacons and smoke equipment. The weapon's automation selects the appropriate feed channel for the chosen payload.

Energy cycle

The Needle Launcher does not require a separate reactor. Its energy comes from a replaceable power cassette or, in the heavy-duty configuration, from the powered combat suit. The power unit gradually charges the pulse accumulator; the accumulator releases its stored energy to the electromagnetic accelerator in a fraction of a millisecond. The accelerator converts that pulse into the projectile's kinetic energy.

As a result, the system's short-duration peak power can be much higher than the continuous output of its primary energy source.

Thermal operating rule

The main operating constraint is not the number of rounds available, but the heat generated by the electromagnetic accelerator. A phase-change thermal accumulator temporarily absorbs substantial heat without allowing the working components to overheat rapidly.

After intensive firing, the automation may reduce the rate of fire or disable the heavy mode even if ammunition remains and the power unit is not depleted. An overheated launcher is not necessarily useless: it may lose the ability to fire heavy projectiles while remaining capable of launching light needles and low-energy special payloads.

Emergency thermal cartridges

The launcher may use one or more replaceable thermal cartridges to temporarily exceed its normal combat cycle. After use, a cartridge must be replaced or allowed to cool for an extended period. More aggressive heat-dumping methods do not remove the system's underlying thermal limits.

Recoil and sound: practical limits

A heavy penetrator produces substantial recoil. The electromagnetic launch system is not completely silent: the launch cycle, moving mechanisms and impact can still be heard or detected. A high-velocity needle is therefore not an absolutely silent projectile.

Needle Launcher and kharakternyky

For a kharakternyk, the launcher reaches its full potential through integration with the thought-link and powered combat suit. Thought-link provides information from the weapon and aiming systems during combat. The suit can also supply additional energy and support more powerful operating modes.

The fighter, powered suit, thought-link, sensors and launcher consequently operate as one integrated combat system.

System limitations

The Needle Launcher is versatile, but it is not limitless. Payload mass, required acceleration, energy reserves, thermal capacity, cassette type and the target's protection all constrain its use. A special payload does not automatically inherit the performance of a light needle.
