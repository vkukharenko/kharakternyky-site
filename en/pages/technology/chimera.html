TECHNOLOGY 03 · COMBAT SYSTEMS

Chimera

An active multispectral camouflage system for a combat platform.

Chimera is not an invisibility system. Its purpose is to turn a combat machine's precise, stable signature into a larger, unstable and partially transparent area, making it harder for enemy sensors to determine the target's exact position, class and trajectory.

Chimera's active camouflage creates a controlled, optically active aerosol region around or in front of the combat platform. It changes the conditions of observation rather than making the machine's physical presence disappear.

The model uses a Series R combat robot as its base platform: an approximately two-metre humanoid machine with multispectral sensors, an infrared channel, lidar/rangefinding, audio sensors and a tactical interface.

What Chimera masks

Complete invisibility across all bands is practically unattainable. Movement, heat, exhaust, turbulence, noise, reflections and radio signals still leave traces. The system therefore works by increasing uncertainty in observation.

  • Visible spectrum — scattering, absorption and local changes in light transmission.
  • Near infrared — reduced contrast and stability of the visual-infrared image.
  • Thermal infrared — partial blurring and displacement of the thermal signature.
  • Millimetre-wave / radar — partial disruption of radar-image stability.

The aim is to increase spatial and classification uncertainty, break stable tracking and force the observation system to reacquire the target.
