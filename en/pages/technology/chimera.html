TECHNOLOGY 03 · COMBAT SYSTEMS

Chimera

An active multispectral camouflage system for the Chimera combat platform.

Chimera is not an invisibility system. Its purpose is to turn a combat machine's precise, stable signature into a larger, unstable and partially transparent area, making it harder for enemy sensors to determine the target's exact position, class and trajectory.

The system creates a controlled, optically active aerosol region around or in front of the combat platform. It changes the conditions of observation rather than switching off the machine's physical presence.

The model uses a Series R combat robot as its base carrier: an approximately two-metre humanoid platform with multispectral sensors, an infrared channel, lidar/rangefinding, audio sensors and a tactical interface.

What Chimera masks

Complete invisibility across all bands is practically unattainable. Movement, heat, exhaust, turbulence, noise, reflections and radio emissions still leave traces. The system therefore works by increasing uncertainty in observation.

  • Visible spectrum: scattering, absorption and local changes in light transmission.
  • Near infrared: reduced contrast and stability of the visual-infrared image.
  • Thermal infrared: partial blurring and displacement of the thermal signature.
  • Millimetre-wave / radar: partial disruption of radar-image stability.

The aim is to increase spatial and classification uncertainty, break stable tracking and force the observation system to reacquire the target.

System construction

Chimera is not a single smoke generator. It is a coordinated assembly comprising a mixture-storage system, feed lines, nozzles, control units and sensor feedback.

Aerosol system

Forms a controlled region from particles of different sizes and composition. Nozzle geometry and airflow determine its shape and lifetime.

Electrical control

An electric field is used to adjust the movement of charged particles. It does not suspend the cloud in the air or cancel aerodynamics.

Sensor loop

Onboard sensors monitor the camouflage region because the system also interferes with the platform's own observation.

Observation management

Local viewing windows, separated sensors, alternative spectral channels and the ability to thin the region locally allow the platform to observe through its own camouflage.

Operating modes

Close-range layer

A thin layer approximately 10–40 cm around the body. It uses the least mixture and energy and is intended for local signature reduction.

Primary combat mode

A controlled region between the robot and enemy sensors. Model dimensions: about 2–3 m wide and high, 0.3–0.8 m thick, positioned approximately 0.5–1.5 m from the body.

Short enhanced mode

Lasts approximately 3–15 seconds. Used when crossing an exposed area, disrupting laser or radar tracking, or rapidly changing the tactical situation.

Extended controlled region

The camouflage mixture is projected more than 1.5 m from the platform to create an aerosol obstacle farther ahead.

Working-region dimensions

Mixture composition

The hard-SF model uses a composite mixture with components intended to operate across different observation bands:

  • components for visible and near-infrared wavelengths;
  • components for thermal infrared;
  • components for millimetre-wave and radar observation;
  • particle charging and control systems.

In the visible spectrum, the mixture should not turn the region into an opaque black wall. Its purpose is to create unstable scattering, local changes in light transmission and background distortion.

Consumption and reserves

The amount of mixture available limits the duration and intensity of operation. Close-range and short enhanced modes consume different amounts; sustained use of a large region rapidly increases consumption. The system is therefore intended for controlled, task-specific activation rather than permanent operation at maximum output.

Wind and aerodynamics

The most important external factor is not absolute wind speed alone, but the relative airflow between the platform, the camouflage region and the surrounding environment.

Modelled region retention: 50–70%.

5–8+ m/s

A stable local layer becomes doubtful.

Chimera therefore does not create a universal screen regardless of conditions. Nozzle geometry, particle size, airflow and short operating periods are critical to its effectiveness.

Power consumption and heat

The system must dissipate not only the heat generated by its electronics, but also the thermal load associated with generating and controlling the camouflage region. Continuous operation as a solid “cocoon” is therefore not its baseline mode.

Own observation

Chimera creates obstacles not only for the enemy. If the machine completely encloses itself in the camouflage region, its own sensors also lose information.

The design therefore includes sector-based viewing windows, separated sensors, alternative spectral channels, drone assistance and local thinning of the region in front of the sensors.

Limitations

  • The system does not make the platform invisible.
  • Strong relative airflow can destroy the local region.
  • Thermal and other physical traces do not disappear completely.
  • The platform's own sensors may partially lose observation quality.
  • A trained opponent may recognise characteristic signs of the system in operation.
  • Long, continuous camouflage greatly increases mixture consumption and thermal load.

Tactical role

Within the hard-SF model, Chimera is primarily intended to temporarily degrade enemy observation and target tracking.

Breaking target tracking

Turning a clear target into an unstable region may force the observation system to reacquire it.

Crossing exposed ground

A short enhanced pulse can support rapid movement through an area under intense observation.

Reducing aiming accuracy

The system seeks to increase uncertainty in the target's position and movement, making precise aiming more difficult.

Creating time to manoeuvre

Its purpose is to create a brief opportunity for the platform or its unit to change position.

Use in the Kharakternyky universe

Chimera is a specialised system for a Series R combat platform. It does not replace armour, movement, cover or conventional concealment. It gives the unit a temporary advantage by making the enemy's sensor picture less reliable.

Canonical status

The described dimensions, operating ranges and retention figures are model values for the hard-SF concept. They explain the system's operating logic and should not be treated as immutable engineering specifications unless the story later establishes them as such.
