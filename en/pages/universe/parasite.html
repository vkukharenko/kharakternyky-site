FORCES AND ORGANISATIONS

The Parasite

An energy entity acting through human hosts

NATURE

A single consciousness without a single body

The Parasite is an energy entity with a single consciousness, capable of travelling between spheres of worlds. Within a world, it can distribute its presence among many human hosts.

The hosts are parts of one system, not separate parasites. Through them, the entity perceives events and influences the human world. Losing one host does not necessarily destroy the Parasite, but may deprive it of information, connections or ways to exert influence.

The Parasite feeds on the energy of human suffering. This source sustains its activity, but is not the same as the energy required to carry out its ultimate design.

CONTROL OF HOSTS

Two ways to control a person

Full control — a puppet

The Parasite can suppress or destroy a person's consciousness and control their body against their will. Under this form of possession, the person no longer makes their own decisions.

Personal memories may be preserved. The Parasite can then use the host's memories, knowledge, skills and relationships, while the person's behaviour may appear entirely normal. Preserving memories, however, does not mean the original personality is still present.

Partial control — hidden influence

The person's identity remains intact, but the Parasite can steer decisions, direct attention and amplify particular emotions or motives. The person continues to believe they are acting independently and may not realise they are being influenced.

This allows the Parasite to shape events through people without turning them into obvious puppets.

The Parasite can use both modes within the same network: hidden influence where a host's independence and cover matter, and full control where unquestioning execution is required.

MIND-LINK ANALOGUE

A hidden communications network

The Parasite can provide controlled people with its own analogue of the characterники mind-link — an energy mechanism for communication and coordination between hosts.

Communication between hosts

The mechanism allows people to coordinate without ordinary communication tools and receive instructions through the Parasite's network.

Coordinated action

Scattered hosts can act in concert even if they do not know one another in everyday life or see the larger design.

This is an analogue of the mind-link, not necessarily the same mechanism. Its interaction with the genuine mind-link used by characterники has not yet been defined.

NETWORK OF INFLUENCE

A network without an open headquarters

Hosts under hidden influence

People whose personalities remain intact. The Parasite uses their motives, fears, resentment and ambition to steer their decisions.

Fully controlled agents

People whose consciousness has been taken over. They can carry out tasks for which the Parasite does not want to rely on a host's independent judgement.

Hosts with a mind-link analogue

They enable covert coordination and connect separate actions into a unified network.

A single consciousness

One Parasite stands behind every part of the network. People whose actions serve it may know neither that it exists nor what it ultimately intends.

LIMITS

Distributed does not mean omniscient

The Parasite receives information through its hosts and does not necessarily see everything beyond its network. Human choices can be unpredictable, and losing an important host may cut off access to valuable knowledge or connections.

Blind spots

It has no automatic access to every human thought or event; its understanding depends on the information available to it.

Network losses

A host's death or exposure may break an important connection or deprive the Parasite of specific capabilities. Rebuilding the network is not necessarily immediate.

The exact limits of full control, the possibility of resistance by characterники, and the cost of controlling many people simultaneously still need to be defined in the canon.

TRUE PURPOSE

The destruction of humanity

The Parasite's ultimate goal is to destroy all of humanity. To achieve this, it is preparing a chain reaction intended to engulf the entire planet in a short time. Human conflicts, decisions and actions may be parts of the preparation, even though people cannot see the unified design.

Humanity's destruction is meant to release enough energy to open paths to the next sphere of worlds. The energy of suffering therefore feeds the Parasite and sustains its activity, while planetary annihilation is the final step of its plan.
