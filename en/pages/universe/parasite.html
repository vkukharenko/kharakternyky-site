FORCES AND ORGANISATIONS

The Parasite

A single consciousness hidden among humanity

OVERVIEW

A consciousness without a single body

The Parasite is not a creature that can be found in one place. It is a single consciousness distributed across many human hosts.

The hosts are parts of one system, not independent parasites. Through them, it perceives events and influences the human world. Losing an individual host does not necessarily destroy it, but may deprive it of information, connections or ways to exert influence.

The Parasite’s true nature is concealed. People may see individual actions and their consequences without realising that one distributed consciousness stands behind them.

HOW IT INFLUENCES PEOPLE

Its methods

Uses human motives

The Parasite amplifies existing fears, resentment, ambition and convictions. People often believe they are acting independently and pursuing their own goals.

Needs no single headquarters

Hosts can be spread across different areas of society. They do not necessarily know about one another or understand the combined result of their actions.

Conceals the larger pattern

Individual decisions have ordinary human explanations. Only the combined sequence of events may reveal a pattern that exceeds the interests of any one person.

Feeds on suffering

Human pain and suffering sustain its activity. The true purpose of this process, however, is unknown to humanity.

LIMITS

Neither omniscient nor invulnerable

Its distributed consciousness makes the Parasite resilient to local losses, but not all-powerful. It receives information through its hosts and may remain unaware of events beyond the reach of its network. Human choices remain unpredictable, and losing important hosts can limit its influence.

Blind spots

The Parasite has no automatic access to every human thought or event. Its view of the world depends on what its hosts can perceive.

The cost of losses

A host’s death or exposure may break an important connection or deprive the Parasite of specific capabilities. Rebuilding its network is not necessarily immediate.

UNKNOWN PURPOSE

Beyond human understanding

Humanity does not know the Parasite’s true purpose. Even those whose decisions serve it may not suspect that it exists. From the outside, its influence looks like a collection of human conflicts, ambitions and decisions rather than the work of an openly organised group.

Its long-term design concerns humanity as a whole, not the victory of a single state, ideology or faction. What it is preparing remains hidden.
