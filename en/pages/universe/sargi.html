Sargi

The Sargi (Sarg) are a sentient humanoid race found across many Plans of the Empire. Their members serve in the military and work as mercenaries, guards and special-operations specialists. Keor belongs to this race.

Appearance and physiology

The Sargi have dense, tough skin with prominent bony plates. Their surface resembles natural armour: individual plates overlap to form a protective layer, especially visible on the head, shoulders, chest and limbs. This is anatomy, not equipment.

A Sarg's head is distinctly non-human. The elongated skull is covered with ridged bony plates, with sharp crests above the eyes and along the upper head. The eyes have a dark iris, a vertical pupil and a red glow associated with biological indicators and Imperial implants.

Despite the alien structure of the head, the limbs retain a humanoid layout. The hands have five fingers, stronger than human fingers, with thickened joints and dense nail plates. Each foot has three main weight-bearing toes and an additional rear stabiliser. This structure provides stability when running, jumping and carrying heavy loads.

Without armour, a Sarg stands approximately 20–40 centimetres taller than an average human. The shoulders and rib cage are broader, the musculature more powerful, and the pelvis and feet are built differently. Combat armour makes the silhouette bulkier, but the underlying proportions remain recognisable: it is the same creature, not a human in a suit.

Key traits

Humanoid but non-human physiology; dense skin with bony plates; enhanced strength and endurance; adaptation to different environments.

Imperial implants

Compatibility with communication, translation and enhancement systems. Implants are Imperial equipment, not a natural anatomical feature of the race.

Place in the Empire

Within the Empire, the Sargi perform military and security duties, serve in special units and work as mercenaries. Their physical endurance and ability to operate in different environments make them suitable for long missions far from their home Plan.

Keor is a member of this race. His mercenary experience, his attitude towards a completed mission and his indifference to the political causes of a conflict fit the professional environment in which he operates. His personal choices do not necessarily represent the views of all Sargi.
