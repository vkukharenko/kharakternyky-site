04 / TRANSITIONS

A transition is a possibility; a portal is how it is used

A transition is a technical term describing the possibility of travelling between two specific Plans. It is not a portal or a route. A portal connects two specific points on Plans linked by a transition.

Two points

If the destination coordinates are known, a portal can open to any specified point on the target Plan. If they are not specified, it opens at the same coordinates as on the current Plan.

Both directions

A transition allows travel in both directions. An unlimited number of portals can be opened between the same pair of Plans.

Sequential route

To reach a distant Plan, all intermediate Plans on the chosen route must be crossed. An ordinary transition cannot skip them.

Portal corridors

Long routes consist of sequential transitions between neighbouring Plans. If one segment stops working, the corridor is broken.

Maintaining and reproducing corridors

A route formed by a characterник can be stored and maintained by special devices. They are not essential: a characterник can maintain the entire corridor personally, but doing so requires considerably more effort.

Any characterник can track a portal opened by another characterник, provided they know where and what to look for. This makes it possible to reproduce an existing portal corridor without travelling through every intermediate Plan or creating the route again.

A person travelling through such a corridor does not notice the intermediate Plans.
