04 / TRANSITIONS

A direct portal leads to a neighbouring Plan

A single portal cannot skip several Plans. If the destination is far away, reaching it requires a sequence of transitions between neighbouring Plans.

Neighbouring Plan

Each direct transition connects two neighbouring links in a branch.

A long route

A distant world is reached through several consecutive transitions. Such a route can be thought of as a portal corridor.

No skipping

An intermediate Plan cannot simply be bypassed by a single portal when it lies on the route.
