A DISTINCT RACE

The Horned

The Horned seek to become the crown race of a Plan. To do so, they must destroy those who already hold that place. On human Plans, their target is humanity.

The decisive condition is simple: if even one human remains on a Plan, the Horned cannot become its crown race. Their struggle is therefore directed not only against organised resistance, but against the continued presence of humans themselves.
